Finding a few unusual seeds does not mean you need to redesign the whole field. Give them a small watered patch, check their season, and decide whether the first harvest is food, a sale, or the start of a larger seed supply.

## Four crops, four planting decisions

| Crop | Season on the main farm | First growth | Regrowth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carrot | Spring | 3 days | None |
| Summer Squash | Summer | 6 days | Every 3 days |
| Broccoli | Fall | 8 days | Every 4 days |
| Powdermelon | Winter | 7 days | None |

The individual references for [Carrot](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Carrot), [Summer Squash](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Summer_Squash), [Broccoli](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Broccoli), and [Powdermelon Seeds](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Powdermelon_Seeds) list their growth and acquisition rules. These are crop seeds, not the seasonal Wild Seed packets that grow forage.

## Finding a seed does not make it plantable today

Some seed acquisition windows extend into the preceding season. A packet found near a season boundary may be preparation for next month. Read the packet's planting season before placing it in the ground; the date of discovery is not permission to ignore the season.

Keep upcoming seeds in a planting chest rather than mixed with produce. When a new season starts, they will be visible beside your shop-bought packets. If only a few arrive, place them at the edge of a familiar sprinkler block so they are easy to find at harvest.

## Food now or more plants later?

Carrots are a quick Spring food crop. Squash and Broccoli can provide repeated harvests, so planting early gives them more opportunities to earn back the occupied space. Powdermelon lets you maintain a conventional watered crop patch in Winter, but it still needs daily watering or sprinkler coverage.

For a hypothetical six-packet find, plant all six only if the patch fits your available watering. A handful of free seeds is not free labor when you are already struggling to finish the morning.

Once you have a [Seed Maker](/guides/seed-maker-guide/), separate a small expansion reserve from what you intend to eat or sell. Do not put the entire first harvest into the machine automatically: output varies, and the crop you sacrifice had another use.

Use the [seasonal crop guides](/guides/spring-crop-guide/) to plan the rest of the field. For Winter, compare your new patch with the time you intended to spend on [tools, fishing, and farm improvements](/guides/winter-guide/). A smaller planting may leave room for a more useful upgrade.
