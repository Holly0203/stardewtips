Take the scythe and collect the Moss you have a use for. Green Rain changes the valley for a day, but you do not need to clear every weed or cut every unusual tree before bedtime. A route tied to a few recipes is easier to use than a frantic tour of the whole map.

## Recognize the event

Green Rain happens once each Summer in an unmodded 1.6 game, on one of a set of possible dates. Its forecast and appearance differ from an ordinary storm. The [Weather reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Green_Rain) documents the event and temporary changes.

Do not assume the green color is a normal lightning forecast. A plan to gather Moss is different from a plan to produce batteries, and the event should not be used as a guaranteed Battery Pack day.

If the next-day forecast signals the unusual weather, make space in the backpack before sleeping. You can prepare the tools without committing to an all-day expedition.

## Pack for a collection trip

Bring the scythe for the relevant growth and Moss collection. Add an axe only if cutting trees is part of your plan. Keep food and free slots, and leave unrelated building and gift reserves at home.

The [backpack guide](/guides/backpack-storage-guide/) helps distinguish a useful loadout from the habit of carrying every tool. More free slots mean fewer decisions about which ordinary finds to discard halfway through the route.

Before leaving, name the material target. For example, three [Mushroom Logs](/guides/mushroom-log-tree-placement/) need thirty Moss and thirty Hardwood under their standard recipe. Gathering thirty Moss does not also provide the required hardwood; keep both parts visible in the plan.

## Temporary growth is not permanent damage

Large quantities of event weeds and unusual trees appear. The event weeds spawn without destroying existing things and disappear afterward; many altered trees revert once the day has passed.

That is a reason to avoid panic-clearing an established grove just because its appearance changed. If you depend on particular [tapped trees](/guides/tapper-tree-products-guide/), check what the event is doing before chopping them down to “repair” the area.

Collect around the grove and keep access clear. You can decide on a permanent tree-layout change later when the temporary effect is over.

## Choose a route with an endpoint

Start with the farm and an adjacent area you already know. Return when the intended material batch is collected, the pack needs unloading, or the remaining time makes the next leg inconvenient.

If another area looks interesting, visit it because you want to explore, not because missing its final weed will ruin the event. A one-day opportunity can be enjoyable without requiring complete harvesting of every available tile.

Leave enough time to put the materials away. A reserve becomes useful when you can find it again, rather than leaving the next morning's backpack full of an unsorted collection.

## Town does not follow an ordinary first-year day

During Year 1 Green Rain, villagers' behavior changes and gifting is unavailable in current 1.6 patches. Some electronic devices also do not operate normally. Do not build the outing around an ordinary gift route or rely on a phone check that the event has interrupted.

You can still explore the changed conversations and atmosphere. Keep that separate from a plan to gain normal birthday or weekly gift progress. Our [friendship guide](/guides/friendship-guide/) covers the usual system when the event is not overriding it.

If you have a timed request, check what actions remain possible rather than assuming every usual interaction works unchanged.

## Turn the materials into one finished project

After the event, count the actual Moss and place it with the recipe it is meant to fund. Complete that batch if the other ingredients are ready. Keep a small surplus if upcoming recipes need it, but do not expand the equipment plan solely because one material is abundant.

The [crafting guide](/guides/crafting-guide-beginners/) can help choose what belongs next. If Moss is plentiful but other inputs are missing, work on those inputs rather than crafting unnecessary copies of whatever happens to be affordable.

**Reference:** event frequency, temporary growth, first-year interactions, and electronic-device changes checked against the linked Weather page on 24 October 2026. Material targets use ordinary Mushroom Log recipes and do not assume a guaranteed collection yield.
