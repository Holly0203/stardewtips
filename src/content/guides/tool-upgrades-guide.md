Upgrading your tools is one of the most impactful things you can do — a better pickaxe breaks rocks faster, a better watering can waters more tiles, and a better axe finally chops that hardwood stump. Here's how it all works.

## How to upgrade a tool

Take your tool (and the required bars) to **Clint the Blacksmith**, east of town, across the river. Each upgrade costs gold + metal bars, and takes **2 in-game days** to complete.

| Level | Bars required | Gold cost |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| Copper | 5 ![Copper Bar](/images/items/copper-bar.png) Copper Bars | 2,000g |
| Iron | 5 ![Iron Bar](/images/items/iron-bar.png) Iron Bars | 5,000g |
| Gold | 5 ![Gold Bar](/images/items/gold-bar.png) Gold Bars | 10,000g |
| Iridium | 5 ![Iridium Bar](/images/items/iridium-bar.png) Iridium Bars | 25,000g |

> **While Clint works on a tool, you can't use it.** Time your upgrades carefully — don't upgrade your watering can right before a dry spell, or your pickaxe before a big mining day.

## What each upgrade does

### Pickaxe

Breaks rocks faster and can break harder rocks. **Upgrade this early** — it speeds up mining dramatically and lets you go deeper.

### Axe

Chops trees faster and, at Copper+, can **chop hardwood stumps and logs** — required to access the Secret Woods and build certain items. Upgrade to Copper early for hardwood access.

### Watering can and Hoe

Hold the button to **water or till multiple tiles** at once:

- Copper: 3 tiles in a line.
- Iron: 5 tiles.
- Gold: 3x3 area.
- Iridium: larger areas.

The watering can is a huge quality-of-life upgrade — a Gold or Iridium watering can waters a whole 3x3 (or bigger) patch in one action.

## The best order to upgrade

1. **Pickaxe** first — faster mining means faster bars for everything else.
2. **Axe to Copper** — unlock hardwood early (Secret Woods, certain crafting recipes).
3. **Watering can** — big time-saver for farming. Upgrade it on a rainy day or before Winter, when you don't need to water.
4. **Hoe** — useful for tilling large fields fast at the start of each season.
5. **Scythe** — the ![Iridium Scythe](/images/items/iridium-scythe.png) Iridium Scythe (endgame) harvests a larger area.

## Timing tips

- **Upgrade the watering can in Winter** — no crops to water.
- **Upgrade the hoe after planting**, or right before a new season.
- **Use rainy days** — the day before, water your crops, then upgrade the can (rain will water them for you).
- **Upgrade the pickaxe last thing** before you plan to leave the mines alone for a couple days.

## The bottom line

Upgrade your **pickaxe first** for faster mining, get your **axe to Copper** for hardwood, then pour upgrades into the **watering can** for farming efficiency. Always time your upgrades around rain or Winter so you're never stuck without a tool you need.
