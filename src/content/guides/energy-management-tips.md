Energy is the real currency of Stardew Valley — it limits how much you can do each day. Running out mid-afternoon is frustrating, but it's easy to avoid once you know how to manage your stamina.

## How energy works

- **Every tool swing and action costs energy.**
- Your energy bar resets each morning when you wake up.
- **Running out** leaves you exhausted — you move slowly, and if you keep pushing you'll pass out.
- **Sleeping** is the main way to restore energy (a full bar overnight).

> If you pass out at 2am or from exhaustion, you lose gold and wake up groggy. Try to head home before your energy runs dry.

## The best ways to restore energy

### 1. Foraged food (free and easy)

Early game, foraging is your best energy source. Items like Spring Onions, Wild Horseradish, and berries are free and give a small boost.

### 2. Field Snacks — craft them yourself

![Field Snack](/images/items/field-snack.png) Field Snacks are craftable from **foraged seeds** (acorns, pine cones, maple seeds). They restore a solid chunk of energy and cost nothing but the seeds you find lying around.

> Keep a stack of Field Snacks in your inventory. They're the best early-game "food on the go."

### 3. Berry seasons — stock up

Two times a year, berry bushes explode with fruit:

- **Spring (Salmonberry, days 15–18)** — bushes around town drop berries.
- **Fall (Blackberry, days 8–11)** — same, with ![Blackberry](/images/items/blackberry.png) Blackberries.

Spend a day or two shaking bushes and you'll have hundreds of free berries for energy all season.

### 4. Cooked meals

Cooked dishes restore far more energy than raw ingredients. Once you have a kitchen (first house upgrade), cooking becomes your best energy source. ![Coffee](/images/items/coffee.png) Coffee also gives a speed boost.

### 5. The Spa — free full restore

The **Spa** (north of the railroad, opens Summer 3) **fully restores your energy and health for free**. Just walk in and stand still in the pool.

## Smart habits to avoid running out

- **Don't over-water by hand.** Sprinklers automate the biggest daily energy drain.
- **Plan your day.** Do energy-heavy tasks (mining, clearing trees) when your bar is full.
- **Eat before you're empty.** Don't wait until you're exhausted.
- **Upgrade tools** — better tools do more with less energy.
- **Stop before midnight** — being in bed by midnight means a full energy bar next morning (later than that and you wake up with less).

## The bottom line

Energy is manageable: **forage free food, craft Field Snacks, hoard berries during berry seasons, and use the Spa** once it opens. Add sprinklers and tool upgrades on top, and you'll rarely see your energy bar bottom out again.
