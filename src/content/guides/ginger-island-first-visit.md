Take the watering can if entering the Volcano is part of the plan. It has a use there that does not appear in an ordinary mine loadout. If the first visit is only for exploring the island, keep the trip modest instead of trying to finish a new dungeon and every unlock before returning.

This page includes the basic access sequence, but leaves most individual walnut puzzles unspoiled.

## Repair the boat before budgeting daily tickets

After the relevant Community Center or Joja completion, Willy invites you into the shop's back room. The boat repairs require **200 Hardwood, 5 Iridium Bars, and 5 Battery Packs**. Once the overnight repair is complete and access is available, the ordinary boat ticket costs **1,000g**, including the return.

The [Fish Shop's boat section](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Fish_Shop#Willy.27s_Boat) lists the repair requirements. Do not treat paying for a ticket as an alternative to supplying the boat materials.

Separate those reserves from [Stable](/guides/stable-horse-travel/) materials, sprinkler inputs, or another project using the same hardwood and bars. The total requirement is easier to manage when the stacks have one agreed destination.

## Make the first trip affordable to repeat

A ticket payment is an outing cost, not an admission that permanently removes all future travel expense. Keep another trip's budget available if returning soon matters.

For a hypothetical 3,000g travel reserve, ordinary tickets support three visits before considering other spending. That says nothing about the value of the finds or how much progress the island must provide in each visit.

Avoid turning the first day into a repayment target. Learning the map and returning safely is a useful result, even if you have not earned the fare back in saleable items.

## Follow the open path first

On arrival, the initial route leads east toward Leo. The early interactions open further access; other paths need walnut-funded unlocks. The [Ginger Island reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Ginger_Island) describes the sequence and the different regions.

Notice the dock and the route back while the map is still simple. Read each parrot request before spending Golden Walnuts. Reaching a total collected and deciding which unlock to buy are different steps.

If you want to avoid puzzle spoilers, keep your own note of clues and blocked paths instead of reading a complete answer list immediately. The first visit does not need to include every accessible objective.

## Pack for the chosen activity

For exploration, take useful tools, food, and empty slots. For the Volcano, add the watering can, an appropriate weapon, and enough healing food for an unfamiliar area. The can creates a route across lava at the entrance; it is not there merely to water a possible field.

Use the [combat preparation guide](/guides/combat-survival-guide/) for the hotbar arrangement. A regular mine's comfortable floors do not establish that the new enemy mix is equally manageable.

Leave gifts, long-term building reserves, and unrelated crop stacks at home. The [backpack guide](/guides/backpack-storage-guide/) explains why a focused loadout matters especially when a paid trip brings several unfamiliar item types.

## Mainland chores still exist

Before leaving, cover animal feed and any watering the main farm needs. Island progress does not suspend those routines. If you later obtain overnight accommodation, revisit the farm-care plan rather than assuming yesterday's preparation covers several days.

Island weather is separate from the mainland. A rainy forecast at home does not automatically mean an island rain puzzle is available. Check the appropriate report once it is unlocked, or the weather you actually find on arrival.

The [Rain Totem guide](/guides/rain-totem-weather-planning/) explains the same region distinction if weather becomes part of a later plan.

## Leave with the next question answered

Before returning, note what blocked the next step: walnuts, an access request, equipment, or simply time. Keep newly found items separate until you know whether they belong to a donation, recipe, or ordinary sale.

Then prepare the next trip around one of those gaps. That gives a second visit a purpose without requiring you to carry every possible item the first time.

## Give the second trip one unlock target

Before leaving the first visit, write down the next blocked route and the parrot's displayed price. Keep that requirement separate from the walnuts already spent. Total walnuts found over the save and the walnuts still available to buy an unlock are different counts.

If the next trip is for the western farm, pack for clearing and growing. If it is for the Volcano, pack the watering can and recovery supplies. If you want to stay overnight, check the Farmhouse unlock before assuming the dock trip includes accommodation. Choosing one target makes the return bag and travel budget much easier to prepare.

**References:** repair materials and boat access checked against Fish Shop; ticket, initial routes, regional weather, and Volcano can use against Ginger Island, on 24 October 2026. This is a first-visit plan for unmodded 1.6, not a complete walnut or dungeon walkthrough.
