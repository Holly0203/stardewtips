Keep seasonal bundle items before selling the rest of a harvest. Missing a single fish or crop can mean waiting for another season. This page covers standard bundles; a save with remixed bundles needs a different checklist.

## Read the slots, not just the pictures

Some bundles require every listed item. Others show more possibilities than there are slots to fill. Check both the quantity and quality before making a donation. A gold-quality crop stack is not interchangeable with one ordinary crop if the bundle specifies otherwise.

The [standard bundle reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Bundles) lists the exact items, quantities, and individual rewards. Use your save's bundle screen as the immediate checklist, especially if you selected remixed bundles when creating the farm.

Keep a small reserve at home for what you still need. Carry it when you intend to donate, rather than leaving every possible bundle item in the backpack through unrelated trips.

## What completing each room changes

| Room | Room completion reward | Useful planning question |
|---|---|---|
| Crafts Room | Quarry bridge repair | Which seasonal forage is still missing? |
| Pantry | Greenhouse repair | Which crop or animal item needs a longer lead time? |
| Fish Tank | Glittering Boulder removal | Which fish conditions will occur next? |
| Boiler Room | Minecart repair | Can current mining trips supply the missing materials? |
| Bulletin Board | Friendship benefit | Which requests overlap with supplies already being collected? |
| Vault | Bus repair | Can the payments fit without consuming operating money? |

Completing an individual bundle and completing a room are separate reward steps. Claim available bundle rewards rather than assuming they will enter the backpack automatically.

## Seasonal crop checklist for standard bundles

Keep one of each named crop for its seasonal crop bundle, unless another quantity is shown here:

| Season | Crop bundle reserve | Other planting to plan |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | Parsnip, Green Bean, Cauliflower, Potato | Five gold Parsnips if using that Quality Crops set |
| Summer | Tomato, Hot Pepper, Blueberry, Melon | Five gold Melons and/or Corn for Quality Crops |
| Fall | Corn, Eggplant, Pumpkin, Yam | Five gold Pumpkins or Corn for an unfinished quality set |

Quality Crops needs **three of four sets**, each set being five gold-or-better crops: Parsnip, Melon, Pumpkin, Corn. That is fifteen qualifying crops across three chosen sets, not five mixed crops. Corn can cover Summer or Fall planning, but use separate copies for ordinary and quality donations.

Plant a single Bean with access around its trellis; plant the slow Cauliflower early. These are small collection commitments, so you can keep the rest of the field focused on income. The [Spring](/guides/spring-crop-guide/), [Summer](/guides/summer-crop-guide/), and [Fall](/guides/fall-crop-guide/) calendars help place harvests before the season boundary.

## Check fishing conditions ahead of time

For each missing fish, note the water, season, weather, and time. Several fish can share a location while appearing under different conditions. A long trip at the wrong time will not fill the requirement faster.

Use the [fishing guide](/guides/fishing-guide-beginners/) to prepare the rod and a manageable outing. When you land the needed fish, move it to the bundle reserve before the rest of the catch reaches the shipping bin.

Missing one seasonal fish is inconvenient, but it does not mean the save has failed. Continue the rooms you can progress and keep the missed condition on the next-season list.

## Three ways to choose the next room

**You regularly mine:** inspect the Boiler Room first. Its standard Blacksmith bundle needs one Copper, Iron, and Gold Bar; Geologist needs Quartz, Earth Crystal, Frozen Tear, and Fire Quartz; Adventurer takes two of Slime (99), Bat Wing (10), Solar Essence (1), and Void Essence (1). That is a precise gathering list for the minecart reward, not a reason to delay a rare seasonal item elsewhere.

**You want Winter growing:** work backward from the Pantry. Crop donations have seasonal windows; animal products need buildings and mature animals. The Animal bundle takes five of six choices, while Artisan takes six of twelve. Choosing a feasible subset can avoid buying an entire production system for one optional slot.

**Your missing tasks are mostly seasonal:** reserve their outputs first, then spend spare effort on the Vault's gold payments. Do not ship the only required crop to bring a cash payment forward by one day. A near-complete room is a useful priority only when its remaining inputs are actually available.

These are standard-bundle rules from the [bundle tables](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Bundles). A remixed save must use its displayed scrolls instead. The [Greenhouse plan](/guides/greenhouse-guide/) shows what the Pantry reward can support once unlocked.

## The Joja alternative

The Joja route exchanges bundle collection for paid development projects after membership. It suits players who prefer earning gold to gathering the specified items. The routes have different payments and rewards, so compare the projects available in your save before committing.

Do not join merely because an ordinary item is missing this week. Equally, you do not have to follow the bundle route if the collection process is not something you enjoy. The important choice is which progression you want to spend time on.

## Before the next town trip

Check the bundle screen, count the exact items in your reserve, and leave space for rewards. If the next missing item needs a crop to mature or an animal to develop, make that a future task instead of packing unrelated supplies.

## Count a bundle reserve before a bulk sale

Use two separate stacks when one crop serves different requirements. One normal Melon can fill the Summer Crops slot, while the Quality Crops choice needs five qualifying Melons. Owning five gold Melons does not mean you can donate all five to both places.

Before the shipping pass, inspect the live scroll, remove the exact reserve into storage, then sell the remainder. A bundle icon pulsing over an item is a useful prompt, but the quantity and quality in the scroll are the deciding details. If a remixed save disagrees with this page's list, follow that save's actual slots.
