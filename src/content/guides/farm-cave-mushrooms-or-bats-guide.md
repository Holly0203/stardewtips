Choose fruit bats if extra chances at fruit would fill a gap in your bundles or gifting supplies. Choose mushrooms if you want the boxes’ collection routine and the included Dehydrator. Demetrius’s offer is permanent in normal play, but neither choice prevents you obtaining the other resources elsewhere.

Neither choice guarantees that every future ingredient will appear exactly when you need it. Treat the cave as a supporting system rather than the single foundation of your whole farm plan.

> **At a glance**
>
> - The offer follows **25,000g in total earnings**, not a requirement to hold that much cash.
> - Choose between fruit bats and mushrooms.
> - The choice is permanent in normal play.
> - Fruit variety can help collection goals, but individual drops are uncertain.
> - Check your game's patch before using older guides for mushroom production timing.

## Separate the unlock from the spending budget

The [Farm Cave reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/The_Cave) identifies the total-earnings trigger. Reaching it does not mean you have a spare 25,000g available for orchard purchases or equipment.

Look at the money and resources actually on hand when making the decision. If your farm still has several urgent purchases, the cave can complement those constraints, but it does not erase them.

It is also reasonable to make the choice based on an activity you enjoy. A small recurring collection that fits your routine can be more valuable to you than a theoretical advantage from something you regularly forget to visit.

## Consider bats when fruit variety fills a gap

Fruit bats can leave different fruits in the cave, including some fruit-tree products. That can make them useful when you want additional opportunities for bundle or gifting items before investing in every sapling.

The key word is opportunities. Do not assume one specific fruit will appear by a particular date merely because it is on the possible-output list. If a deadline matters, consider a source you can plan more directly as well.

Our [Community Center guide](/guides/community-center-guide/) helps identify what is actually missing from your bundles. Check that list before valuing every possible fruit equally; the best cave output for your save is often the item that closes a real gap.

## Consider mushrooms as an ingredient routine

The mushroom option adds six boxes that produce every other day in 1.6, and includes a free Dehydrator. Mushroom varieties are still random, so a predictable collection schedule is not a guarantee of a specific ingredient. Evaluate it by whether those ingredients fit the food or processing routine you want to build.

Before choosing based on a precise daily-income claim, check the current source and your game version. Older advice about production schedules can be misleading when patches change behavior.

If the attraction is supplying food, inspect the actual ingredients and recipes you can use. “More mushrooms” does not automatically mean every useful cooked dish is unlocked or that every variety should be eaten directly.

Our [cooking guide](/guides/cooking-and-food-buffs-guide/) explains how to separate ingredients, learned recipes, and food effects.

## Match the cave to the rest of the farm

Imagine two different saves. One player is already buying fruit saplings and wants a consistent habit of checking ingredients. Another is delaying the orchard and values extra chances at several fruits. They can reasonably make different choices without either farm being ruined.

The comparison should include your other sources, not just the cave in isolation. If a resource is already abundant elsewhere, getting more of it from the cave may solve less than the alternative does.

Use the [fruit tree guide](/guides/fruit-tree-planting-guide/) to compare a planned orchard with the uncertainty of bat drops. A tree requires a bigger commitment, but its role in the calendar is different from a random cave find.

## Leave collection space and keep reserves purposeful

For the bat option, uncollected fruit and objects occupying the floor reduce available spawning space. Keep the cave's role clear instead of turning every spare tile into general storage and then wondering why fruit opportunities feel limited.

For either choice, move outputs into a system you understand: reserve current bundle items, set aside ingredients you will use, and decide what the rest is for. The cave should not become an unsorted second pantry simply because it is out of sight.

If you repeatedly forget to visit, tie the cave check to another farm task. An easy existing route is more dependable than adding an isolated chore to the end of a long day.

## You can still build a balanced farm afterward

Choosing one option does not remove the rest of the game's ways to obtain fruit or mushrooms. A missing item may call for another source rather than regret about a decision made seasons earlier.
