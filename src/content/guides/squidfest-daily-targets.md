Eight squid reach the top SquidFest tier on Winter 12. The same total does not reach the top tier on Winter 13. Read the daily sign and claim each day's rewards before assuming yesterday's result carries forward.

## The daily thresholds

SquidFest takes place at the Beach on Winter 12–13, from 6:10am to 2am. Squid are available all day during the event. The catch counter resets between days.

| Tier | Winter 12 | Winter 13 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copper | 1 squid | 2 squid |
| Iron | 3 | 5 |
| Gold | 5 | 7 |
| Iridium | 8 | 10 |

The [SquidFest reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/SquidFest) lists the reward bundles and their possible variations. Count catches against the event display; carrying old squid into the event is not a substitute for catching them there.

## Pick a useful stopping point

Choose a target before the first cast. If you only have half a day, a lower tier can be a complete plan. Once you reach it, decide whether the next threshold is close enough to justify staying.

For example, being one squid short of a chosen tier is a different situation from beginning a new tier late at night. Base the decision on remaining time and how reliably you are landing fish, rather than how much time you have already spent.

The clock keeps moving, and animals still need food. Winter does not remove the morning feeding job. Check the [hay guide](/guides/silo-hay-winter-feeding-guide/) if your usual outdoor grazing routine has stopped working.

## Bring the solution to your fishing problem

Prepare bait and any replacement tackle before heading to the Beach. For players who struggle to keep squid on the bar, a control aid can be more useful than equipment chosen only to speed up bites. The [tackle comparison](/guides/bait-and-tackle-guide/) explains the options without assuming you have a late-game rod.

If you own a [Bait Maker](/guides/bait-maker-targeted-fishing/), converting an early squid can help target subsequent catches. Keep enough space to collect both the bait and your event prizes.

Claim rewards with room in your bag: overflow can drop on the ground. Before going home on the first day, restock for the second and choose its target separately. This is a short fishing event within your [Winter schedule](/guides/winter-guide/), so leave yourself time for the upgrades you planned before the festival arrived.
