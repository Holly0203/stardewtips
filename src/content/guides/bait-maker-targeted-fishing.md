Targeted bait helps you find more of a fish you have already caught. It does not make an out-of-season fish available, and sacrificing a unique catch is a poor way to discover that limitation.

## What goes into one batch

The Bait Maker recipe unlocks at Fishing level 6 and uses three Iron Bars, three Coral, and one Sea Urchin. One fish produces five to ten matching bait in about ten in-game minutes. See the [Bait Maker reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Bait_Maker) for accepted inputs and processing rules.

Check the fish's name before loading. Keep legendary catches and collection specimens in a different chest. Making bait from a legendary fish you already caught does not allow another ordinary catch of that fish.

## Conditions still come first

[Targeted Bait](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Targeted_Bait) increases the chance of the named fish only where the fish is currently catchable. Location, season, weather, and time remain relevant. It also requires a rod that accepts bait; the Bamboo Pole and Training Rod cannot use it.

Before a fishing session, check four things: your water location, the date, the weather, and the current hour. If one condition is wrong, changing the bait will not fix the session. Once the conditions match, targeting can make repeated catches more productive.

## When the sacrificed fish is worthwhile

A delivery asking for several of one species gives you a clear reason to use the machine. So does an event where more of the same fish advances a reward. If you only need one fish for a bundle and already hold it, processing that fish creates a new problem instead of solving one.

Suppose you catch an ordinary target early in its availability window. Converting it may leave enough time to use the resulting bait. Catching it in the last few minutes of the window is different: store the bait for another eligible session rather than expecting it to extend the deadline.

Try one batch first. A better hook chance does not guarantee every cast will produce the target, and lost fish still cost time. If the minigame is the bottleneck, improve control with the [tackle guide](/guides/bait-and-tackle-guide/).

Use the machine alongside the [Trout Derby](/guides/trout-derby-tags-plan/) or [SquidFest](/guides/squidfest-daily-targets/) plans when repeat catches matter. Keep ordinary bait available too, so running out of targeted bait does not end a useful fishing trip.
