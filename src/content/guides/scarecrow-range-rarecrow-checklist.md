A regular Scarecrow protects much farther than the eight adjacent tiles, but not every corner of a large square. Check its actual range shape before surrounding the field with unnecessary duplicates or assuming a far corner is covered.

Rarecrows use the same ordinary protection range. Their different appearances are a collection feature, not eight increasingly powerful protection tiers.

## Read radius as a shape

The [Scarecrow reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Scarecrow) shows the ordinary range: eight spaces in the cardinal directions, tapering at the corners. It is a seventeen-by-seventeen bounding area with ten tiles excluded from each corner.

That means counting eight tiles straight east does not prove the tile eight east and eight north is protected. The diagonal corner is farther away. Use the diagram and inspect the field outline rather than treating radius as a fully covered square.

For a long narrow field, one placement may work differently from the same crop count arranged in a wide rectangle. Crop count alone is therefore not enough to choose the number of scarecrows.

## Place it on its own tile

A Scarecrow and a sprinkler cannot occupy the same tile. Reserve both positions when drawing the field. A decorative path can help make the equipment locations readable, but it does not erase their footprint.

Start from the [sprinkler pattern](/guides/sprinkler-guide/), then place crop protection so the outer planted tiles fall within range. Avoid solving coverage by pushing a scarecrow into a harvesting path you need every morning.

If you change the field shape, inspect the edges again. A layout that covered last season's plot may leave new tiles outside range even though the scarecrow itself has not moved.

## What the eight Rarecrows contribute

Collecting all eight unlocks the Deluxe Scarecrow recipe through a later letter. The [Deluxe Scarecrow entry](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Deluxe_Scarecrow) lists the recipe as **50 Wood, 1 Iridium Ore, and 40 Fiber**, with twice the ordinary radius.

The recipe letter may arrive on a subsequent night rather than immediately on the screen where you obtain the last Rarecrow. Keep the collection and check mail before assuming the game has failed to recognize it.

A larger radius can simplify a developed field. It does not mean the first-year farm needs to finish every festival and collection milestone before planting ordinary crops.

## Make the collection list by source

Use the full Rarecrow list in the ordinary Scarecrow reference and group missing pieces into practical categories:

- Festival purchases to remember before starting the activity that ends the event.
- Museum rewards to claim after the relevant donation milestone.
- Purchases from areas you have not yet unlocked.

That grouping is more useful than attempting to finish the full list during one town trip. Reserve the currency needed for a planned purchase, and distinguish event tokens from ordinary gold.

The [Fair guide](/guides/stardew-valley-fair-grange-guide/) and [Museum guide](/guides/museum-donation-guide/) cover two of those systems without treating their rewards as interchangeable.

## Where an extra scarecrow is unnecessary

The Greenhouse does not need crow protection, and Ginger Island has no crows. Ordinary Wild Seed crops also have an exemption, as documented in the reference.

Those are specific exceptions. They do not mean an unprotected field of ordinary seasonal crops is safe because it stands next to forage. Check the crop actually growing in each area.

If the goal is decoration, place a Rarecrow because you like it. Just keep that decision separate from a calculation that assumes each display item is providing necessary new coverage.

## Before buying more copies

Count the missing collection types, then examine the current field's uncovered tiles. These are different reasons to acquire a scarecrow. A duplicate might be useful for a second field but will not fill a missing type in the eight-piece collection.

When expanding, plant the boundary you intend to use and recheck the range diagram. That small inspection is easier than discovering several harvests later that the prettiest corner of the field was outside protection.

**References:** ordinary shape, Rarecrow behavior, and exemptions checked against Scarecrow; recipe and unlock timing against Deluxe Scarecrow, on 24 October 2026. Coverage advice uses unmodified range and separate equipment tiles.
