If your invitation is refused at the Flower Dance, try checking friendship rather than repeatedly asking. An eligible dance partner normally needs at least four hearts. A friendly conversation that morning does not replace the missing relationship progress.

## The festival deadline

The Flower Dance takes place on Spring 24 in Cindersap Forest. Entry is from 9am to 2pm, and you return home at 10pm. Speak with an eligible bachelor or bachelorette through their normal dialogue before asking them to dance. The [Flower Dance reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Flower_Dance) documents the four-heart requirement and the friendship gained from dancing.

This is easier to plan over several weeks than to fix on the festival morning. Choose someone you actually want to befriend, check their schedule, and carry suitable gifts while doing nearby errands. The [friendship guide](/guides/friendship-guide/) explains the regular interactions that build toward the threshold.

## A first-year invitation is an optional project

During your first Spring, money and daylight also go toward seeds, fishing equipment, and mine progress. Pursuing a dance partner is reasonable, but make the cost visible. A long detour every day can matter more than the price of the gift itself.

Use the [gift guide](/guides/best-gifts-villagers/) to choose items you can reliably obtain. Reserving a small supply is more practical than discovering each morning that yesterday's gift was the last one. Check friendship before the final week so you know whether the invitation is plausible.

If you are short of four hearts, you can still attend. Talking with villagers and seeing the event does not require a successful dance invitation. Keep improving the relationship afterward rather than treating the refusal as a lost save file.

## Shop before starting the dance

Leave spending room if you collect Rarecrows. The festival sells Rarecrow number 5 for 2,500g. Check the stall and finish conversations before asking Lewis to begin the dance.

Do essential farm care before attending because of the late return. Pack the intended shopping money into your budget without consuming the gold reserved for the next planting. The [Spring crop plan](/guides/spring-crop-guide/) helps identify what spending is already committed.

After the event, resume ordinary friendship habits. Dancing is a useful increase, but a relationship still grows through the rest of the season. A plan that fits your normal town route is easier to maintain than a schedule built entirely around one festival date.

## Check progress before the final festival week

Use the Social tab before Spring 24 to confirm the intended partner is actually at four hearts. If the relationship is below the threshold, count suitable remaining conversations and gifts without treating every interaction as a guaranteed full heart.

A birthday before the dance can make focused preparation useful, but the item still needs to match the recipient and ordinary gift rules. Keep the dance plan limited to the person you want to befriend. Repeatedly buying gifts for several candidates on the final day will not replace weeks of missing relationship progress.
