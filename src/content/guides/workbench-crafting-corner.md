Leave a place to stand beside your Workbench. Surrounding all eight neighboring tiles with chests maximizes theoretical storage while blocking the player from using the bench.

## What the bench can reach

Robin sells the Workbench for 2,000g. It draws ingredients from neighboring ordinary Chests and Big Chests, including diagonal neighbors. It does not pull through a chain of containers or use Junimo Chests. The [Workbench reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Workbench) explains its eight-tile reach and ingredient search behavior.

Start with fewer chests than the maximum. Place the bench beside a clear aisle, add nearby materials storage, and walk up to use it before filling the area with decoration or machines. A comfortable approach is more valuable than another mostly empty container.

## Store materials by how you use them

Keep common construction ingredients together, with ores and bars close enough for recipes that use both. Put unrelated sale items elsewhere. The purpose is to craft without carrying half the workshop, not to make every object on the farm part of one large storage pile.

For example, a chest of Wood and Stone and a second chest of bars can both supply one recipe when adjacent. If a needed ingredient is in a container two tiles away, the fact that another chest connects them does not extend the range.

Use colors consistently. A visible distinction between crafting stock and shipping stock reduces mistakes when you return tired with a full backpack. The [storage guide](/guides/backpack-storage-guide/) offers a broader system if your current sorting rules have become hard to remember.

## When a recipe is unexpectedly unavailable

Check the recipe unlock first, then ingredient counts, then chest placement. The bench provides access to materials; it does not teach recipes. Open nearby chests to confirm the missing item has not been moved into an out-of-range container.

Quality and item identity can also matter for some crafting requirements. Read the recipe rather than substituting an item because its icon looks similar. Keep a few test crafts small until you trust the arrangement.

## Expand capacity before adding awkward reach

If the layout works but storage is full, a [Big Chest upgrade](/guides/big-chest-upgrade-planning/) can add slots without changing the bench's footprint. Buying more capacity is useful only when the contents belong there, so remove obsolete sale stock first.

Check the [crafting priorities guide](/guides/crafting-guide-beginners/) before producing a large batch. Easy ingredient access makes it convenient to overspend shared materials. Reserve bars for the next tool or machine you actually need, then craft against the remaining supply.

## A reachable three-by-three corner

```text
C C C
C W C
. P .
```

Here **W** is the Workbench, **C** an adjacent Chest, **P** the approach position, and dots open ground. Diagonal chests count; a chest beyond this three-by-three neighborhood does not become reachable through another chest.

This five-chest example leaves the front open rather than surrounding all eight neighbors. Stand on P and try one small craft before moving the rest of the materials. If the recipe is learned and the needed item still appears missing, open the containers and check their actual placement instead of adding another row of storage.
