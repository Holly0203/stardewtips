If eggs are scattered across the coop, an Auto-Petter will not collect them. If your animals are being neglected, an Auto-Grabber will not improve their care. The names sound like a matched automation set, but each machine replaces a different action.

| Machine | Replaces | Still your responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auto-Grabber | Collecting supported animal products inside its building | Feeding, petting, outdoor truffles |
| Auto-Petter | Daily automatic petting inside its building | Feeding and collecting products |

The [Auto-Grabber](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Auto-Grabber) costs 25,000g at Marnie's after Farming level 10. The [Auto-Petter](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Auto-Petter) has a guaranteed 50,000g Joja purchase route after the development form is complete; other sources involve random rewards. Do not make an animal expansion depend on finding one in your next cavern run.

## Choose one building to improve

Start with the building that interrupts your morning most. A busy coop can mean many individual pickups. A barn with milk and wool means repeated tool use. Place the collector inside that building, then check its inventory as part of your processing route.

An Auto-Grabber stores 36 stacks. Eggs, milk, wool, and their quality variants can occupy different slots, so “I only have a few animals” is not a reason to stop checking it. Put processing equipment close enough that emptying the collector does not turn into a cross-farm delivery.

The collector does not award the normal product-collection experience or the friendship gained from manual milking and shearing. While raising a new herd or building Farming levels, that trade deserves consideration.

## Automation does not fill a feeder

Before buying either machine, inspect [hay storage and feeding](/guides/silo-hay-winter-feeding-guide/). A collector cannot compensate for an empty bench. Automatic petting also does not prevent neglect caused by missed food.

Manual petting can still add friendship alongside the Auto-Petter. You can use it as insurance on travel days while continuing hands-on care when home. The practical benefit is flexibility, rather than a reason to stop entering the building entirely.

## Pigs need another plan

Truffles appear outdoors and are not gathered by the Auto-Grabber. Leave a walkable collection area and check it yourself. If pigs are your main source of income, buying collectors for every barn may save less time than you expect.

Use the [animal guide](/guides/animals-guide/) to compare the whole building's workload. After collection is under control, check [cheese and mayonnaise processing](/guides/cheese-mayonnaise-quality-rules/) before spending more on machines that do not address the remaining bottleneck.
