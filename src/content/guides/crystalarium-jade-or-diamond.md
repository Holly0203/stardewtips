Put Jade in a Crystalarium if the aim is a regular supply of Staircases. Put a Diamond in if straightforward sale income is more useful. The gem with the highest individual price is not automatically the right choice for a machine meant to support a specific trip.

## Check acquisition before spending iridium

The crafting recipe unlocks at **Mining level 9** and uses **99 Stone, 5 Gold Bars, 2 Iridium Bars, and 1 Battery Pack**. A Crystalarium is also a standard Vault bundle reward and a Museum reward for fifty mineral donations.

The [Crystalarium reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Crystalarium) lists those routes. A reward machine can begin a small copying plan without first funding the recipe's full material bill. If a reward is close, compare finishing that goal with spending the iridium now.

Our [Museum guide](/guides/museum-donation-guide/) explains donation milestones; the [Community Center guide](/guides/community-center-guide/) distinguishes the Vault from the other rooms.

## One inserted gem starts repeated copying

The machine retains the selected gem and keeps producing copies. It does not need another raw gem for each cycle. Prismatic Shards cannot be copied.

In 1.6, changing the inserted gem requires removing and replacing the machine; the inserted item can be recovered when the machine is removed. Finish or deliberately abandon the current cycle before reorganizing a whole copying area.

Label a group by purpose if you use several gem types. That makes it easier to recognize which output is a trip supply and which can safely join the sale pile.

## Jade has a use beyond its sale price

The [Desert Trader](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Desert_Trader) exchanges **one Jade for one Staircase on Sundays**. A supply plan therefore includes the trading day and desert access, not just the machine timer.

For example, a reserve of twelve Jade can become twelve Staircases at that exchange. That says nothing about how deep the next cavern run will reach: travel, placement time, enemies, and the supplies used after descending still matter.

Store trade Jade outside the ordinary sale reserve. Accidentally shipping a prepared stack can undo several copying cycles even when the gold payment looks useful at first.

## Compare Diamond income by time

A Diamond has a **750g base sale value** and a roughly **five-day copying cycle**. That is a simple baseline of **150g per machine-day**, assuming collection and refilling behavior do not introduce delays and no sale bonus applies.

The ratio is useful for comparing a sale-oriented machine, not for pricing a missing staircase at its own shipping value. Jade solves an equipment-supply problem; a Diamond gives money. Which is preferable depends on whether you need that equipment or the money now.

If iridium is still scarce, consider the alternative use of the two bars in a newly crafted machine. A [sprinkler](/guides/sprinkler-guide/) or tool improvement may address a daily constraint sooner than copying income repays the investment.

## Do not interrupt the plan with constant switching

A machine that changes jobs every time you read another strategy can spend a surprising amount of time with unfinished output. Choose the next use first: perhaps enough Jade for the upcoming trade, then a sale gem afterward.

Count what is already in storage before expanding. If several Staircase stacks are unused because you have not yet prepared a cavern outing, more copying is not the missing step. Work on the [Skull Cavern loadout](/guides/skull-cavern-guide/) instead.

For a sale setup, move ready gems into a clearly marked sale stack so they do not get confused with donation or gift reserves. You do not need an individual chest beside every machine; one understandable reserve can serve a small group.

## When a different mineral is reasonable

A quest, gift routine, or crafting project can justify copying something other than the default money or staircase choices. Check that the item is accepted and that the expected cycle fits the deadline.

Do not sacrifice a unique or needed first find on the assumption that the machine accepts everything. Read the item description and compatibility list before inserting it. For a new donation, decide whether Museum progress or repeated access matters first.

## Work backward from Sunday

Jade's listed copy time is about **one day and sixteen hours**, rather than the roughly five-day Diamond cycle. Collection timing still matters: a ready copy waiting in the machine is not another cycle already underway. See the [Crystalarium output table](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Crystalarium).

For a Sunday trade target, count Jade already stored first, then copies you can collect before leaving. If you need ten and already own eight, two available machines may close the gap without building a permanent new row. Keep the resulting Staircases with the cavern supplies so next week's trade calculation does not count them as missing again.
