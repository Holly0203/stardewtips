A Tea Sapling's base sale price is **250g in 1.6**, not the 500g quoted in older strategies. Selling a batch can still be useful, but compare the new payment with the Wild Seeds, Fiber, and Wood you are giving up. If you plant it, the return comes from a bush with a seasonal harvest window instead.

## Get the recipe, not just two hearts

Caroline mails the recipe after her **two-heart event**. Reaching the heart threshold and seeing the event are separate steps. If the recipe has not arrived, check whether the event was actually triggered before trying to craft.

Each sapling uses **2 Wild Seeds, 5 Fiber, and 5 Wood**. The Wild Seeds are the seasonal forage seed packets, not ordinary crop seeds or Mixed Seeds. The [Tea Sapling reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Tea_Sapling) lists the requirements and growth rules.

Use the [friendship guide](/guides/friendship-guide/) if you are still working toward Caroline's event. There is no need to carry a large gift collection for the entire town just to pursue this one unlock.

## A batch sale is not all profit

Using the listed recipe and base sale price, ten saplings require:

| Component | Ten-sapling total |
|---|---:|
| Wild Seeds | 20 packets |
| Fiber | 50 |
| Wood | 50 |
| Gross sale | 2,500g |

The 2,500g is gross income. The seeds and materials have other uses, and gathering them takes time. If you need those same packets for forage crops or Fiber for another recipe, count the trade rather than calling the batch free money.

For a cash gap, make only the number you intend to sell. Crafting every possible packet can leave the next planting or equipment project short of the same ingredients.

## What planting changes

A Tea Bush takes **twenty days** to mature and does not need watering. Once mature, it produces Tea Leaves during days **22–28** of Spring, Summer, and Fall. Indoor bushes can also produce during Winter's final week.

That calendar means a planted bush is not an immediate replacement for selling its sapling. Count the days until both maturity and a qualifying harvest window. A late-season planting may have to wait before it contributes leaves.

A short row can be attractive when you want a low-watering permanent feature. Place it where the collection route will remain accessible. You are still making a layout decision, even though watering is absent.

## Plan the access before making a hedge

Tea bushes can serve as a barrier. That is useful only if there is a route through or around it where you need one. Leave room for a gate or an open passage before enclosing machines or an animal entrance.

If tea is mainly decorative, a few plants are enough to establish the look without committing every available crafting material.

## Keep leaves separate from the sapling decision

Selling saplings, growing leaves, and processing those leaves are three different calculations. Do not price a planted hedge by multiplying today's sapling sale price by every future harvest, or assume a leaf's processed value is received immediately.

If the processing queue already struggles with crops, consider whether tea adds a comfortable task. The [keg and jar comparison](/guides/keg-vs-preserves-jar/) explains equipment time and why a new input needs its own available capacity.

Store any leaves you actually want for a recipe or another use before moving the rest into the sale or processing pile. A permanent plant should not create an indefinite unsorted reserve.

## Which choice fits this week?

Sell a modest crafted batch when cash is the priority and ingredients are genuinely spare. Plant a few when you want continuing leaf production or a useful hedge and can wait through the calendar. Keep the ingredients when they are already assigned to a more urgent job.

## Trigger Caroline’s event before waiting for mail

At two hearts with Caroline, enter her sunroom through the kitchen door in Pierre's home between **9am and 5pm on a non-rainy day**. The recipe arrives after the event; merely reaching two hearts does not create it in the crafting menu. See [Caroline's event conditions](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Caroline#Two_Hearts).

When the mail arrives, inspect Wild Seed stock before crafting. Seasonal forage packets qualify; Mixed Seeds and ordinary vegetable packets do not. If the batch is for sale, use the current 250g base price instead of an older 500g calculation. If planting, count maturity and the final-week harvest window separately.
