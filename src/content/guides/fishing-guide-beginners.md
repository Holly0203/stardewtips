Fishing is one of the most frustrating skills for new players — and one of the most rewarding once it clicks. This guide walks you through the minigame, the gear, and exactly where to cast for the best catches.

## How the fishing minigame works

When a fish bites, you enter a minigame with two bars:

- The **green bar** is your hook, which you control.
- The **fish icon** moves up and down, and you must keep it inside the green bar.

**Tap or hold the button to raise the bar, release to lower it.** The bar has a little momentum, so tapping lightly gives you more control than mashing.

> The key skill is feathering the button — many short taps keep the bar steady better than long holds.

## The gear you need

| Rod | Cost | Requirement | What it does |
|-----|------|-------------|--------------|
| Bamboo Pole | Free (from Willy) | None | Basic rod |
| Training Rod | 25g | None | Bigger bar, easy fish only — great for beginners |
| Fiberglass Rod | 1,800g | Fishing level 2 | Can use bait |
| Iridium Rod | 7,500g | Fishing level 6 | Can use bait and tackle |

**Start with the Training Rod.** It only catches basic fish, but the larger green bar makes the minigame far more forgiving. Switch to the Fiberglass Rod once you're comfortable.

### Bait and tackle

- **Bait** (from ![Bug Meat](/images/items/bug-meat.png) Bug Meat, or Willy's shop) makes fish bite faster.
- **Tackle** (Iridium Rod only) adds special effects — the **Trap Bobber** slows fish escape, and the **Cork Bobber** enlarges your bar.

## Leveling up and making money

Every catch earns Fishing XP. Each level increases your bar size, and at higher levels you catch better fish with less effort.

- Sell your catches for solid early-game gold.
- Keep fish the Community Center needs — many bundles require specific species.
- **Treasure chests** appear during the minigame; hold the bar steady over the chest to grab loot, then back onto the fish.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Holding the button constantly** — the bar overshoots and bounces. Tap instead.
- **Ignoring the Training Rod** — it's the fastest way to build skill without frustration.
- **Fishing only in one spot** — different fish (and prices) are everywhere.
- **Selling every fish** — check the Community Center first.

## The bottom line

Fishing is worth mastering early: it's the **best source of Spring income**, it feeds the Community Center, and it never stops paying. Start with the Training Rod, tap instead of hold, and within a season you'll be pulling in catches with ease.
