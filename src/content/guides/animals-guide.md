Animals turn your farm into a steady source of income: eggs, milk, wool, and truffles you can sell or turn into artisan goods. Here's how to get started.

## Coop vs Barn

There are two animal buildings, each with three upgrade tiers:

| Building | Cost | Animals |
|----------|------|---------|
| Coop | 4,000g + wood + stone | Chickens, Ducks, Rabbits, Dinosaurs |
| Barn | 6,000g + wood + stone | Cows, Goats, Sheep, Pigs |

Both can be upgraded (Big and Deluxe) to hold more animals and add **auto-feeding**.

> Start with a **Coop and chickens** — they're cheap and produce ![Egg](/images/items/egg.png) eggs every day. Add a **Barn with cows** for ![Milk](/images/items/milk.png) milk when you can afford it.

## Buying animals

Animals are bought from **Marnie's Ranch** (south of your farm):

- **Chicken** — 800g
- **Cow** — 1,500g
- **Duck** — 1,200g
- **Goat** — 4,000g
- **Rabbit** — 8,000g
- **Sheep** — 8,000g
- **Pig** — 16,000g

## Feeding your animals

- Build a **Silo** first — it stores hay cut from grass.
- **Let animals outside** to eat grass on sunny days.
- **In winter and rain**, they eat hay from the Silo (Deluxe buildings auto-feed).
- **Pet them every day** to raise their hearts — higher hearts mean higher-quality products.

> Build the Silo *before* the Coop. Without stored hay, you'll have to buy it from Marnie every day in winter.

## The best animals for money

1. **Pigs** are the single most profitable animal — they dig up Truffles outdoors, and Truffle Oil sells for a fortune.
2. **Cows and goats** are great steady income when their milk is turned into cheese.
3. **Chickens** are the cheapest entry point and pay for themselves quickly.

## The bottom line

Start small with a **Coop and chickens**, add a **Silo first** for hay, pet your animals daily, and upgrade toward **Pigs** for Truffles. Turn products into artisan goods (Mayonnaise, Cheese, Truffle Oil) to multiply their value, and your animals will become a reliable daily income stream.
