Opening the passage to the Dwarf does not teach the language. To understand Dwarvish and use the normal shop interaction, donate **all four different Dwarf Scrolls** to the Museum and obtain the translation reward.

If you already have several scrolls, check their numerals and colors before starting another search. Four copies of Scroll I are still only one part of that requirement.

## Two separate obstacles

The Dwarf's mine passage can be opened with at least a Steel Pickaxe or a suitable bomb. Beyond it, translation is a separate unlock. The [Dwarf entry](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Dwarf) explains both restrictions.

If you can reach the character but cannot make purchases normally, check the donation progress rather than upgrading the pickaxe again. Spending bars on another tool tier does not fill a missing scroll slot.

Our [tool guide](/guides/tool-upgrades-guide/) covers the ordinary upgrade path. If Museum progress is the issue, the [donation guide](/guides/museum-donation-guide/) explains how to separate new finds from duplicates.

## Search for the missing scroll, not “any scroll”

The [Dwarvish Translation Guide reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Dwarvish_Translation_Guide) lists these broad routes:

| Scroll | Useful source distinction |
|---|---|
| I, red | Mine dirt and several monster drops |
| II, green | Dirt on ordinary mine floors 1–39 and certain enemies |
| III, blue | Monster drops, including Blue Slimes |
| IV, yellow | Dirt on floors 80+ and various monster drops |

These are source directions, not guaranteed finds. Check the detailed entry before designing a repeated route around a particular enemy.

If only Scroll II is missing, spending the whole day tilling deep-floor dirt is not following the listed dirt source for that scroll. Matching the missing numeral to the route is a more useful change than simply spending more hours searching.

## Pack for the source you chose

Take a Hoe if tilling is part of the plan, plus the usual pickaxe, weapon, and recovery food for a mine outing. Leaving the Hoe at home is sensible for some trips; it is not sensible for this particular dirt-search job.

For a monster route, choose floors you can fight on comfortably. A rare drop attempt is not worth repeatedly losing the items you already brought home. The [combat guide](/guides/combat-survival-guide/) covers food access and retreat decisions.

Keep enough empty slots for the target and ordinary finds. If a duplicate appears, read its numeral before deciding whether to keep it. Similar sprites are easy to misread in a full backpack.

## Donate the four types and claim the reward

Once a new type is found, place it in the donation reserve. It should not sit in a general sale or gifting stack while the remaining search continues.

After all four different scrolls are donated, collect the Dwarvish Translation Guide from Gunther's rewards. In 1.6 it is recorded in the Special Items & Powers tab rather than occupying a normal backpack slot.

A missing physical inventory item therefore does not prove the reward was lost. Check the relevant menu and whether the reward was actually claimed.

## What to do with duplicates

Keep a duplicate only if it has a named use for your save. Do not count extra copies as future progress toward the four-type donation requirement.

If you have accumulated a large number of one color, use that as a prompt to review the search rather than a reason to repeat it indefinitely. The missing type may need a different source, or simply more independent attempts on the correct route.

There is no reliable “find it on the next visit” promise in this plan. You can combine attempts with ore or [geode collection](/guides/geode-processing-guide/) so a session still contributes even when the scroll does not appear.
