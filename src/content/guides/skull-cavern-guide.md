The Skull Cavern is the endgame dungeon of Stardew Valley — an endless descent packed with ![Iridium Ore](/images/items/iridium-ore.png) Iridium, ![Prismatic Shard](/images/items/prismatic-shard.png) Prismatic Shards, and treasure. It's also much harder than the regular mines. Here's how to conquer it.

## How to unlock the Skull Cavern

The Skull Cavern is in the Calico Desert, which you reach by **repairing the bus**:

- **Community Center route:** complete the Vault bundles (42,500g total).
- **Joja route:** pay Joja to repair the bus.

Once the bus runs, ride it to the Desert and head to the cave in the northwest.

## What makes it different

- **No elevator.** Every run starts at floor 1 — you descend as far as you can in one day.
- **Floors are infinite.** The deeper you go, the more Iridium and treasure spawn.
- **Tougher enemies.** Serpents, Mummies, Iridium Bats, and more hit hard.
- **You need your own ladders** — or bombs to clear floors quickly.

> Get the **Galaxy Sword** by holding a ![Prismatic Shard](/images/items/prismatic-shard.png) Prismatic Shard between the three pillars in the Desert. It's a massive power jump.

## The strategy for deep floors

1. **Go on a good luck day.** Check the Fortune Teller on TV — a "very happy" or "showered with luck" day means more ladders and treasure.
2. **Eat luck food.** Spicy Eel and Lucky Lunch boost your luck, which speeds up finding ladders and holes.
3. **Bomb clusters of rocks** instead of mining one by one. Holes (which drop you multiple floors) are the fastest way down.
4. **Use staircases** to skip infested floors or slow layouts. You can trade ![Jade](/images/items/jade.png) Jade for staircases at the Desert Trader on Sundays.
5. **Don't fight unless you have to.** Your goal is descending, not clearing every enemy.

## Enemies to watch for

- **Serpents** — flying enemies that charge you. Swing as they approach.
- **Mummies** — they collapse when "killed" but revive; finish them with a **bomb** to keep them down.
- **Iridium Bats and Crabs** — tanky, high-damage enemies deeper down.

## What you're farming for

- ![Iridium Ore](/images/items/iridium-ore.png) **Iridium Ore** — for the best tools, sprinklers, and bars.
- ![Prismatic Shard](/images/items/prismatic-shard.png) **Prismatic Shards** — for the Galaxy Sword, enchantments, and rare gifts.
- **Treasure rooms** — every 10 floors (random) holds a chest with rare loot.

## The bottom line

The Skull Cavern rewards **preparation and speed**. Go on a lucky day, bring bombs and staircases, eat luck food, and focus on descending rather than fighting. Master that loop and you'll come back with a backpack full of Iridium and Prismatic Shards.
