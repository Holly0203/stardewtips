A gem forge and an enchantment spend different rare ingredients. Decide which improvement you want before putting materials into the Forge, especially if you only have one Prismatic Shard.

## Two separate spending decisions

| Action | Main extra ingredient | Cinder Shard cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First weapon gem forge | Chosen compatible gem | 10 |
| Second weapon gem forge | Chosen compatible gem | 15 |
| Third weapon gem forge | Chosen compatible gem | 20 |
| Enchantment | Prismatic Shard | 20 |

Three normal gem-forge steps therefore require 45 Cinder Shards. An enchantment is a separate 20-shard expense. The [Forge reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Forge) explains compatible items, gem effects, and special cases such as Diamond forging.

For an initial budget, keep the 45 and 20 in separate mental piles. Having enough shards for one action does not mean you can finish every planned upgrade on the same visit.

## Improve the weapon you will keep using

A Ruby forge increases weapon damage; an Emerald improves speed. Which feels more useful depends on how you fight and the weapon you already have. If you frequently mistime slow swings, more damage alone may not solve the problem.

Try the current weapon in ordinary combat first. Identify whether enemies survive too many hits, attacks feel too slow, or you are taking avoidable contact damage. The last problem may require better positioning and healing rather than another forge.

The [combat guide](/guides/combat-survival-guide/) can help diagnose that before you spend resources. Do not upgrade a temporary weapon solely because you reached the Forge with gems in your pocket.

## Enchantments are not a shopping menu

A Prismatic Shard enchantment produces a random eligible result. Applying another replaces the existing enchantment; it does not stack an unlimited series of effects. Reserve materials for another attempt only if you are willing to accept that uncertainty.

Before enchanting a tool, think about its actual workload. A property useful during long watering sessions may matter less after your fields have complete sprinkler coverage. Your farm's current chores are a better guide than a generic ranking.

Unlock the [Volcano shortcut](/guides/volcano-dungeon-first-forge-route/) so you can return later. There is no need to complete all improvements during the first ascent. Keep essential healing and a safe exit while gathering more shards, and use your [island travel plan](/guides/island-farm-overnight-plan/) to leave enough time for both collecting and spending materials.
