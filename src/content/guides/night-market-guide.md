The Night Market looks like a relaxed evening event, but its attractions run on different schedules and the clock keeps moving. If you spend too long browsing before heading to the submarine, the trip can become hurried before you even start fishing.

Plan each visit around one main activity. Three nights give you room to spread the experience instead of trying to fit every boat, shop, and catch into one evening.

> **At a glance**
>
> - The event runs on **Winter 15–17** at the Beach.
> - Market hours are **5pm–2am**; the submarine entry closes at **11pm**.
> - The submarine ride costs **1,000g**.
> - Time continues during the event, so budget travel and attraction time.
> - Complete animal feeding and ordinary farm chores before leaving.

## Give each night a priority

Choose whether the visit is mainly for shopping, the Mermaid Show, or submarine fishing. A second activity can fit if time allows, but the main goal should determine the order.

A reasonable personal plan might use the first night to inspect shops, the second for a prepared fishing session, and the third for something missed. This is a suggested itinerary, not a fixed event requirement. Rotating shop stock can make your own priorities different.

The [Night Market reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Night_Market) lists attractions and their schedules. Check the relevant attraction rather than assuming every boat shares the market's latest closing time.

## Do not treat this like an all-day paused festival

Ordinary chores still matter, including feeding animals. Finish those before the evening so you are not deciding between the last submarine entry and an unfinished feeding bench.

Our [hay and silo guide](/guides/silo-hay-winter-feeding-guide/) explains the Winter food routine. A market visit is easier when you can leave the farm knowing that routine is complete.

Pack at home rather than rearranging everything after reaching the docks. Leave room for purchases and catches. A full backpack can shorten the activity even when the clock and your energy would allow more.

## Prepare the submarine session before paying

Take a suitable rod and the supplies you intend to use. The Training Rod cannot catch the three distinctive submarine fish in current 1.6 behavior, so do not bring it expecting a workaround for their difficulty.

The relevant catches include **Midnight Squid, Spook Fish, and Blobfish**. You are paying for attempts, not purchasing a guarantee that every target will appear and be landed during that ride.

Our [bait and tackle guide](/guides/bait-and-tackle-guide/) can help you choose a setup for the part of fishing you find difficult. A paid time-limited session is a poor place to discover that an attachment does not fit the rod you brought.

## Leave time for the return

The ride's descent and ascent each take approximately **thirty in-game minutes**. Do not treat the time when you stop fishing as the time when you are already walking home.

Choose your own departure margin before the session becomes absorbing. Include the return route and any shopping you still intend to finish. If you have a valid return item, confirm that it is actually in the pack and usable rather than relying on a memory of owning one.

The best fishing duration is not automatically the latest possible cast. Ending with your catches safely stored can be more useful than squeezing in one more attempt and creating a difficult trip home.

## Make the Mermaid visit a separate experience

The Mermaid Boat has an earlier closing time than the full market, and the show itself takes time. If watching it is the main attraction, go there deliberately rather than treating it as a spare-minute activity after fishing.

After the performance there is a shell-note puzzle associated with a Secret Note. This guide leaves the sequence unspoiled; the linked event reference includes the answer if you want it. Visiting for the atmosphere is also a complete reason to go.

Do not assume a repeated performance means a repeatable version of every special reward. Check the reward rule before planning multiple evenings around the same payout.

## Shop with a seasonal plan

Before buying seeds, think about where and when they will be grown. A Winter purchase is not automatically an outdoor Winter planting opportunity. Label future-season packets in storage so they do not get mixed into the next immediate chore.

Our [Greenhouse guide](/guides/greenhouse-guide/) and [Winter guide](/guides/winter-guide/) can help you distinguish current growing space from preparation for Spring.

Set a spending limit that includes the ride and anything needed afterward. An interesting decoration is easier to enjoy when it does not consume the money reserved for your next farm project.

## A short checklist before leaving the farm

Finish feeding, choose tonight's priority, pack the appropriate equipment, check space, and decide how you will get home. That turns the event from a frantic checklist into the evening outing it is meant to be.

If one goal remains unfinished, use another night when available rather than cramming everything into the final minutes. A missed catch is not a reason to ignore the clock.
