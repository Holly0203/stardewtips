Winter can feel like a dead season — no regular crops grow in the snow. But it's actually the perfect time to catch up on everything you ignored all year. Here's what to do.

## Winter crops: the wild seeds

Most crops won't grow in Winter, but **Winter Seeds** will. You can craft them from the four foraged winter items (Crystal Fruit, Crocus, Snow Yam, Winter Root), which you'll find scattered around the map.

- Forage these four items.
- Craft them into **Winter Seeds**.
- Plant, harvest, and use the harvest to craft *more* seeds.

It's a self-sustaining loop that turns winter foraging into a decent little crop cycle.

## Foraging

Winter foraging items are worth good money and feed your Winter Seeds:

- **Crystal Fruit**, **Crocus**, **Snow Yam**, **Winter Root** — found on the ground around town and the map.
- **Beach foraging** continues too — shells and coral still spawn.

## Fishing

Fishing doesn't stop in Winter, and some fish are **exclusive to winter**:

- The ![Glacierfish](/images/items/glacierfish.png) **Glacierfish** (legendary) is winter-only.
- River and lake fish still bite all season.

## Mining (the best winter activity)

With no crops to tend, Winter is the **ideal time to mine**:

- Stockpile ores for tool upgrades.
- Gather gems to sell or gift.
- Take on the **Skull Cavern** for Iridium and Prismatic Shards.

## Tool upgrades

Upgrade your **watering can** in Winter — you don't need it! It's the perfect time to upgrade all your tools without missing anything.

- **Watering can** and **Hoe** first (you're not using them).
- **Pickaxe** and **Axe** whenever you have the bars.

## Prepare for Spring

Use Winter to set up next year:

- **Clear debris** and plan your Spring layout.
- **Build and upgrade buildings** (Coop, Barn, Shed).
- **Craft sprinklers** so you're ready to plant on Spring 1.
- **Stockpile seeds and gold** for the Spring seed rush.
- **Process artisan goods** from any crops you stored in the fall.

## The bottom line

Winter is a **reset and prepare** season. Forage for Winter Seeds, mine for ores, upgrade your tools, and get everything ready for a huge Spring. Treat it as maintenance season and you'll come out stronger in Year 2.
