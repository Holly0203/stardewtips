The first Egg Festival combines a social event, a minigame, and one of Spring's most tempting seed purchases. The easy mistake is to prepare for only the shopping: buy more packets than you can plant and water comfortably, or start the Egg Hunt before visiting the stall.

A useful festival plan begins the day before, with a field size and spending limit you can actually support.

> **At a glance**
>
> - The festival is on **Spring 13** in Pelican Town.
> - Enter between **9am and 2pm**.
> - Strawberry Seeds cost **100g each**.
> - Shop and talk before starting the Egg Hunt with Lewis.
> - Leave planting time and energy for the evening if you want to plant immediately.

## Decide how many plants belong on your farm

Use watering capacity, not just available gold, to set the first limit. A new field creates a chore for every remaining growing day. If the current morning already feels full, buying every packet you can afford may make the rest of Spring less enjoyable.

For a sample budget, ten seeds cost **1,000g**, twenty cost **2,000g**, and thirty cost **3,000g**. Set aside money for other immediate needs before choosing one of those scales.

Our [Spring crop guide](/guides/spring-crop-guide/) compares the wider planting options. Strawberries can be a useful addition without becoming the only crop or the only goal of the season.

## Finish ordinary chores before entering

Water existing crops and check your evening plan first. Leave space in the backpack for purchases. If possible, prepare a manageable planting area without exhausting yourself before the event.

The [Egg Festival reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Egg_Festival) explains that the event returns you to the farm at **10pm**. That is enough reason to avoid assuming there will be an ordinary full evening after the social activities.

Do not depend on selling an item to Pierre during the festival to fund the seeds; the festival shop does not buy your produce. Arrange the money beforehand so the budget is real when you arrive.

## Shop before starting the hunt

Talking to Lewis to begin the Egg Hunt advances the event toward its end. Buy the seeds and finish any conversations you want first. Treat the activity trigger as the final decision, not the first stop after entering town.

If you care about winning, be aware that odd and even years use different egg layouts. A memorized route from a different year may not match what is in front of you.

The festival is still worthwhile if the hunt goes badly. The seed purchase and social time are separate benefits, so one minigame result does not undo the rest of the day's plan.

## A simple planting-calendar example

The [Strawberry reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Strawberry) gives an eight-day first growth period and four-day regrowth. With no speed bonuses, a seed planted and watered on Spring 13 is expected to harvest on **Spring 21**, then **Spring 25**, assuming uninterrupted watering and no crop loss.

Planting on Spring 14 instead shifts those dates to **Spring 22 and 26**. This illustrates why a missed festival-evening planting is not automatically the end of a useful strawberry season. Check your own bonuses before applying a plain calendar to a faster-growing setup.

Keep the plants after the first harvest: they regrow. Do not treat them like a one-harvest crop and clear the field immediately.

## Know what the simple profit example includes

Normal-quality Strawberries have a **120g** base price. Under the two-harvest, one-fruit-per-harvest assumption, one 100g seed returns **240g gross**, leaving **140g above seed cost**. Ten plants give **2,400g gross** and **1,400g above seed cost** in that simplified model.

That is not a total labor or opportunity-cost calculation. It excludes professions, quality, possible extra fruit, processing, growth bonuses, and what you could have done with the same time or tiles. Use it as a clear baseline rather than a guaranteed final shipping total.

The [Year 1 income guide](/guides/make-money-year-one/) explains why a good investment still needs to fit the farm's cash flow.

## Save future packets deliberately

You may decide to reserve some seeds for a later planting opportunity instead of using every packet now. Keep that reserve separate from the batch you mean to plant immediately.

Do not accidentally count stored future seeds as part of this Spring's income forecast. A packet in a chest and a watered plant in the field are different stages of the plan.

After the festival, return to the normal routine: maintain the field, harvest on time, and leave room for fishing or exploring. Our [energy guide](/guides/energy-management-tips/) can help keep the extra watering from consuming the whole day.
