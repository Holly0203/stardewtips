One Worm Bin does not supply an unlimited crab-pot route. Count the bait you use each day before deciding how many bins you need, and keep a reserve for days when you forget collection.

## Daily production

A standard Worm Bin produces four or five Bait each morning without another input. Its recipe unlocks at Fishing level 4 and uses 15 Hardwood, one Gold Bar, one Iron Bar, and 50 Fiber. The [Worm Bin reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Worm_Bin) documents the current recipe; older guides may list a different skill requirement.

A Deluxe Worm Bin produces four or five Deluxe Bait daily. It unlocks at Fishing level 8 and consumes a Worm Bin plus 30 Moss to craft. This is a bait-quality upgrade, not an increase in daily item count. See the [Deluxe Worm Bin reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Deluxe_Worm_Bin).

## Do the small supply calculation

If you rebait eight pots every day, one standard bin leaves a deficit of three or four bait daily. Two bins cover those eight uses at their minimum combined output. Rod fishing adds variable demand, so a system balanced exactly around pots may still need purchased or crafted bait.

That example assumes your pots require bait under your current profession. Check your [fishing profession](/guides/fishing-professions-guide/) before building around a job your character no longer has to do.

Keep a modest buffer stack near the route. Surplus production can cover an extra fishing session or a missed collection, while a system with no buffer forces frequent supply checks.

## Convenience matters more than a hidden corner

Place bins somewhere you already visit. A machine far from your ordinary route may produce enough on paper while leaving you short at the water. Near the pot collection entrance or a fishing supply chest is often more useful than an otherwise empty corner of the farm.

Keep the setup small until you know you will maintain the [crab-pot route](/guides/crab-pot-route-guide/). If you only collect occasionally, tying up Hardwood and bars in many bins may solve a shortage you do not actually have.

For rod fishing, choose bait by purpose. Deluxe Bait can be useful, but it does not replace the species targeting of a [Bait Maker](/guides/bait-maker-targeted-fishing/). Decide whether you need a steady general supply or help catching one particular fish. They are different reasons to build equipment, even when both machines sit beside the same chest.
