A row of Garden Pots can keep a cooking ingredient close to the kitchen. A room packed with them can also become another watering chore. Decide how you will reach and water the back row before planting anything valuable.

## What changes when you bring a crop inside

Indoor pots ignore the outdoor season. Outside on the main farm, seasonal restrictions still apply. Ancient Seeds cannot be planted in a Garden Pot, so this is not a substitute for an Ancient Fruit greenhouse.

Sprinklers do not water pots. Deluxe Retaining Soil can remove repeated watering once you have watered the planted pot initially. Ordinary retaining soils offer a chance to keep moisture, which leaves an inspection job. The [Garden Pot reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Garden_Pot) documents these restrictions and Evelyn's recipe event after the Greenhouse is repaired.

For a first installation, put down a few pots rather than filling a shed. Pick a crop you actually need, water it, and check whether reaching every pot feels comfortable. Expand after a harvest, when you know the arrangement works.

## A layout you can service

Leave a continuous walking aisle. A pot that fits behind another object is not necessarily a pot you can harvest conveniently. Test the layout with empty pots and your normal movement controls before spending seeds or fertilizer.

Group plants by watering needs. Tea bushes, for example, do not need the daily watering that ordinary crops do. Mixing them through a hand-watered room makes the visual check harder than it needs to be. See the [tea planting guide](/guides/tea-saplings-growing-or-selling/) before dedicating a room to them.

Do not move a planted pot as a way to rescue the crop. Removing the pot destroys what is growing in it. Wait for a suitable harvest or accept the replacement cost before rearranging furniture.

## When a small room beats a large one

Suppose your problem is a missing ingredient for a meal. A few dependable plants may solve it; a large production room adds more seed handling and harvest sorting without improving dinner. If your goal is bulk sales, compare the pot materials and access space with the [Greenhouse](/guides/greenhouse-guide/) you already own.

Keep spare seeds beside the room, but leave selling stock elsewhere. That makes replanting straightforward and reduces the chance of processing the last replacement crop. For outdoor automation, use the separate [sprinkler guide](/guides/sprinkler-guide/); the watering rules are different here.
