Upgrade the chest that is difficult to sort before upgrading every chest on the farm. A Big Chest helps when many distinct items crowd one location; it does less for a chest holding only a few large stacks.

## What the upgrade buys

A Big Chest holds 70 item slots, compared with a regular chest's 36. Robin sells the crafting recipe for 5,000g; each crafted chest uses 120 Wood and two Copper Bars. Placing one onto a regular Chest or Stone Chest replaces it while preserving the contents and returning the old chest. These details are listed in the [Big Chest reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Big_Chest).

Capacity is about occupied slots, not just total objects. Crops split by type and quality can fill storage faster than a large pile of one resource. Inspect the contents before deciding whether the problem is limited capacity or an unclear sorting system.

## A sensible first location

A central crafting area is a strong candidate because many different ingredients need to remain available together. A seasonal produce chest can also benefit if you keep cooking, gifting, and replanting reserves in one place.

By contrast, a temporary chest beside a machine may only hold one input and one output. Spending bars there adds capacity you will rarely use. Keep the small chest until its actual contents justify expansion.

For example, three planned upgrades cost 360 Wood and six Copper Bars after purchasing the recipe. Compare that with your next building and tool needs. Upgrade one busy location first if those materials are already committed.

## Replace, then inspect

Choose a quiet point in the day. Apply the Big Chest to the existing storage, collect the displaced chest, and open the new container to confirm your materials are where expected. Leave room around the area so the returned object is easy to retrieve.

Color the new chest to match its purpose. More slots should not remove the distinction between things you mean to sell and things you mean to keep. Put a small sale chest elsewhere if accidental shipping has become a problem.

A [Workbench](/guides/workbench-crafting-corner/) can draw from adjacent Big Chests, so storage expansion can preserve an existing crafting layout. Use the [backpack and storage guide](/guides/backpack-storage-guide/) for the wider sorting system and [crafting guide](/guides/crafting-guide-beginners/) to decide which ingredients deserve permanent space. Empty slots are useful working room; they do not need to be filled with items you have no reason to retain.

## Pick the chest with the most occupied slots

Count slots, not total item quantity. A chest with one large Wood stack and one large Stone stack is using two slots; a crop chest split across species and qualities may be nearly full with far fewer total items.

Upgrade the busy ingredient chest beside a Workbench before replacing every small processing box. After the replacement, open both the storage and the bench crafting interface. If the materials appear in the chest but not at the bench, check adjacency rather than assuming the larger capacity changed the reach. Capacity and crafting access are independent properties.
