A Stardrop permanently raises maximum energy; an ordinary meal restores energy or supplies a temporary effect. If the bar feels small every morning, both systems may matter, but they solve different parts of the problem.

There are seven Stardrop sources. Choose the next one close to your current progress rather than assuming they must be collected in a fixed order.

## The seven-source checklist

The [Stardrop reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Stardrop) lists these sources:

| Source | Requirement to check |
|---|---|
| Stardew Valley Fair | Buy for 2,000 Star Tokens |
| Ordinary Mines | Reach the floor-100 chest |
| Spouse or roommate | Reach the relationship threshold of 12.5 hearts |
| Krobus | Pay 20,000g once the shop is accessible |
| Old Master Cannoli | Give a Sweet Gem Berry in the Secret Woods |
| Willy | Complete Master Angler, then receive the letter |
| Museum | Donate all 95 items |

Each adds **34 maximum energy**. Starting from 270, seven additions give **508**. A Stardrop is consumed when obtained rather than stored as an emergency item for another outing.

Mark a source completed when it has actually been received. A near-finished Museum or a token balance still short of the purchase is progress toward the item, not an energy increase already available.

## Pick by the prerequisite you nearly have

If regular mine progress is near floor 100, continue with food and checkpoint-based trips. Do not spend a large purchase reserve elsewhere solely because the cash source looks faster on a checklist.

If the Fair is approaching, prepare tokens through the event rather than thinking of its 2,000 price as ordinary gold. The [Fair guide](/guides/stardew-valley-fair-grange-guide/) explains the display and event currency.

If Krobus is accessible and money is comfortable, the purchase is direct. If twenty thousand would consume the next planting and feed budget, an immediate larger bar may not be worth that cash gap.

## Cannoli needs a reserved berry

The Secret Woods exchange requires the specified Sweet Gem Berry, not any sweet-looking fruit. Keep one outside the sale stack if this is the next source you want.

The [Rare Seed planting plan](/guides/fall-crop-guide/) explains the long growth window. Buying a seed late in Fall is not the same as having a berry ready for the statue that week.

You also need physical access to the [Secret Woods](/guides/secret-woods-hardwood-guide/). A ready berry does not replace the axe requirement at the entrance.

## Collection sources are longer projects

The Museum and fishing achievements draw on many activities and seasons. Check the actual missing entries before trying to force them into the same short schedule as a shop purchase.

For a Museum gap, identify the item's source and reserve new finds. For fishing, inspect the remaining species and conditions. A large count of repeated common catches will not substitute for distinct missing entries.

Use the [Museum guide](/guides/museum-donation-guide/) and [fishing guide](/guides/fishing-guide-beginners/) to make the next outing specific. You can continue collection progress while the rest of the farm develops.

## Relationships are not a daily resource exchange

The spouse or roommate source belongs to relationship progression. It is not an item you repeatedly buy by giving the same gift after every divorce or change of partner.

If developing that relationship is already part of the save, the Stardrop can arrive alongside it. There is no need to restructure a playthrough around a relationship you do not want merely to match someone else's energy-upgrade order.

The [friendship guide](/guides/friendship-guide/) explains the normal heart progression and why visible caps and relationship stages matter.

## More maximum energy does not fix every short outing

A larger bar supports more tool use, but health, inventory space, and the clock remain separate limits. If combat ends trips, prepare healing. If watering occupies the morning, consider field size and sprinklers. If the bag fills, unload or upgrade it.

The [energy guide](/guides/energy-management-tips/) helps diagnose that recurring cost. A Stardrop is useful progress; it is not a reason to keep a routine that already feels too crowded.

**Reference:** sources, quantities, permanent increase, and consumption behavior checked against the linked Stardrop page on 24 October 2026. Checklist assumes ordinary unmodded progression; multiplayer relationship details have additional rules.
