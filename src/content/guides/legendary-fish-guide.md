Check the required location before spending the day casting. Legendary fish have specific season, weather, skill, and tile requirements, and meeting them only makes a bite possible. Take a setup you can control and leave time for repeated attempts.

## The original five

This table gives the ordinary seasonal requirements. Check the individual location map in the [Fish reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Legendary_Fish) before traveling: broad area names do not show every eligible cast tile.

| Fish | Area | Season | Weather | Minimum Fishing level |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Legend | Mountain Lake near the log | Spring | Rain | 10 |
| Crimsonfish | East Beach pier | Summer | Any | 5 |
| Angler | Waterfall north of JojaMart | Fall | Any | 3 |
| Glacierfish | South end of Arrowhead Island, Cindersap Forest | Winter | Any | 6 |
| Mutant Carp | Sewers | Any | Any | No minimum |

The originals are ordinarily caught once per player, with specific bait-related exceptions. Food can help reach the required effective Fishing level; Legend does not require an additional buff if you already meet level ten.

## Where to stand and where the bobber must land

**Legend:** go to the Mountain Lake in Spring rain. Use the lake islands to reach a bank facing the large submerged log and make a long cast into deep water. The rule is not “hit the log”: the bobber needs a fishing-zone distance of at least four from land, including bridges. Other deep lake tiles also qualify. See the [Legend conditions](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Legend).

**Crimsonfish:** repair the Beach's small bridge with 300 Wood if you have not unlocked that access another way. Cross to the eastern beach and use its pier or shore east of the pier. Cast into open ocean at least three tiles from every land edge, including the pier. Standing beside Willy's ordinary shop pier is not the same location. See [Crimsonfish](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Crimsonfish).

**Angler:** go north of JojaMart to the little wooden plank bridge at the top of the town river. Stand on that bridge and cast into the river. Here your standing position is the important special restriction; there is no minimum water-depth requirement. Check [Angler](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Angler) if fishing from an adjacent bank instead.

**Glacierfish:** enter Cindersap Forest, cross onto Arrowhead Island in the river, and stand on its **single southernmost tip tile**. Face south and cast at least three tiles away from all land. Being one tile back or beside that tip is not the required standing position. The [Glacierfish entry](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Glacierfish) confirms this exact-tile restriction.

**Mutant Carp:** after obtaining the Rusty Key, enter the Sewers through southern town or the forest grate and fish in the water inside. There is no extra special standing tile, season, weather, or minimum-skill requirement. This does not mean the catch is easy: it still uses the minigame. See [Mutant Carp](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Mutant_Carp).

The distance conditions above describe ordinary casts without relying on bubble exceptions. Check that the bobber has landed sufficiently far out before repeating attempts. If a bridge or opposite bank is close, a visually long cast can still be near land on another side.

The [Museum guide](/guides/museum-donation-guide/) explains Rusty Key access. Prepare supplies before a limited weather window, then use the relevant standing position rather than testing random tiles around the whole map.

## Choose support for the difficult part

An Iridium Rod accepts bait and tackle; the later Advanced Iridium Rod accepts an additional tackle. A Trap Bobber or Cork Bobber is worth considering when control is the limiting factor.

Do not rely on the name of a vague “best bait.” Check the attachment you actually own and the slot it uses. Our [bait and tackle guide](/guides/bait-and-tackle-guide/) explains compatibility and the difference between reducing wait time and supporting the catch.

If you use a fishing buff, bring recovery food that will not accidentally replace it with another food buff. The [cooking guide](/guides/cooking-and-food-buffs-guide/) explains that rule.

## A Fishing-level example

A character at Fishing 7 can reach Legend's effective level 10 requirement with **Dish O' The Sea's +3 Fishing**, while the effect lasts. That is eligibility, not a guaranteed hook or catch. Eat it before casting and keep non-buff recovery food for the session; a different buff meal can remove the effect you needed.

Someone already at Fishing 10 does not need that food merely to unlock Legend, though the larger effective skill can still help control. The [cooking guide](/guides/cooking-and-food-buffs-guide/) includes the recipe ingredients and a compatible recovery stack.

## Give yourself several attempts

Ordinary fish may still bite in the target area. That does not by itself prove that the position is wrong. Verify the conditions, then continue if the outing still has time, food, and supplies.

If control is the recurring problem, practice on other catches and return with a more comfortable setup. Fishing level requirements only determine eligibility; they do not promise that the minigame will be easy.

Use the [beginner fishing guide](/guides/fishing-guide-beginners/) for bar control. A missed attempt costs less than carrying an unsuitable setup through an entire limited weather window.

## The later “II” fish follow a different rule

The related fish appear during Mr. Qi's Extended Family quest. Their season and weather requirements are removed, and they can be caught more than once while that quest is active. They are not an annual set unlocked by Leo's relationship storyline.

Keep that quest separate from the original-five checklist. If a later variant appears where you expected an original, check the active quest before changing the whole location plan.
