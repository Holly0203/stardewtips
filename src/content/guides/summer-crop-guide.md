Summer is where your farm starts printing gold. Seeds cost more than Spring's, but regrowing crops like Blueberries and premium picks like Starfruit return several times their price before Summer 28. This guide ranks every Summer crop with real numbers — and clears up a common mistake: **Eggplants are a Fall crop, not a Summer one**, despite what older lists say.

> **At a glance**
>
> - **Backbone crop:** ![Blueberry](/images/items/blueberry.png) Blueberries — 3 berries per harvest, regrowing every 4 days.
> - **Big single payout:** ![Melon](/images/items/melon.png) Melons (250g, and they can merge into Giant Melons).
> - **Premium pick:** ![Starfruit](/images/items/starfruit.png) Starfruit — 400g seed from the Oasis, 750g fruit, 2,250g as wine.
> - **Plant by Summer 15** for 13-day crops (Blueberry, Melon, Starfruit) or they won't harvest before Fall.
> - Summer heat has no gameplay effect, but ![Sunflower](/images/items/sunflower.png) and corn feed bees, chickens, and the Harvest Festival.

## The three money crops

Three crops dominate Summer economics:

| Crop | Seed cost | Sell price | Grow time | Harvest type |
|------|-----------|------------|-----------|--------------|
| ![Blueberry](/images/items/blueberry.png) Blueberries | 80g | 50g ×3 per harvest | 13 days | Regrows every 4 days |
| ![Melon](/images/items/melon.png) Melons | 80g | 250g | 12 days | Single harvest |
| ![Starfruit](/images/items/starfruit.png) Starfruit | 400g (Oasis) | 750g | 13 days | Single harvest |

### Blueberries: the set-and-forget earner

Blueberries are the best-value Summer crop for most players. Each harvest drops **three berries** (150g), and a plant picked on Summer 14 keeps giving a new trio every 4 days — a seed planted on Day 1 pays out roughly **four full harvests, about 600g of fruit for an 80g seed**. No other crop gives you that much return per square of soil without any artisan processing.

They're also a Community Center bundle item and loved by most villagers as a gift — see the [best gifts guide](/guides/best-gifts-villagers/) before you ship your first batch.

### Melons: giant crops and kegs

Melons sell for 250g raw, but their real value is in two tricks:

1. **Giant crops.** Plant Melons in a 3×3 block with no gaps and they have a chance to merge into a Giant Melon, which yields several melons when chopped with an axe. Layout details in the [farm layout guide](/guides/farm-layout-tips/).
2. **Kegs.** A Melon in a Keg becomes Melon Wine at triple the base value — 750g per fruit once aged. If you're reading this before you own a Keg, our [Year 1 money plan](/guides/make-money-year-one/) shows how to get the Community Center's kegs early.

### Starfruit: the premium payoff

Starfruit is the most valuable ordinary Summer crop, with one catch: the 400g seeds are sold only at the **Oasis shop in Calico Desert**, and the desert bus needs repairing first — via the Community Center's vault or Joja. Reachable in Year 1 if you rush bundles; otherwise a Year 2 goal.

The math is brutal in the best way: 400g seed → 750g fruit → **2,250g Starfruit Wine** in a Keg. Nothing else in Summer comes close per tile, which is why endgame farms replace almost everything with Starfruit rows.

**Hops deserve their own paragraph.** Once mature they regrow *every single day*, so a row of them floods your silo. Raw they're worthless; run through a Keg they become **Pale Ale at about 300g each** — easily Summer's highest artisan income if you have the kegs to spare.

**Corn and Wheat** grow through the end of Fall, so a Summer planting costs you no Fall space and feeds animals, bees, and the Harvest Festival.

## What to plant in Year 1

1. **Buy every Blueberry seed you can afford on Summer 1.** They're the backbone of the season.
2. **Add Melons** with spare gold if you plan a 3×3 giant-crop block or already ship via Kegs.
3. **Skip Starfruit** unless the bus is already repaired — 400g per seed is ruinous if you rebuy seeds each Spring.
4. **Interplant cheap filler:** Tomatoes, Radishes, and a few Hops; use Wheat on till you can't be bothered to water.
5. **Remember Eggplants belong to Fall** — buy their seeds in June only if your Fall plans include them.

## The bottom line

**Blueberries are the best Summer crop for most players** — multiple harvests, no processing required, elite profit per tile. **Starfruit is the premium choice** once the desert opens, and **Melons win the giant-crop and wine games**. Plant Blueberries on day one, keep some gold for the Oasis, and you'll roll into Fall with a full wallet — next stop the [Fall crop guide](/guides/fall-crop-guide/).
