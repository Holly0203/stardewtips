Friendship is one of Stardew Valley's core systems. More hearts mean recipes, cutscenes, gifts in the mail, and even marriage. Here's how it all works.

## What hearts mean

Each villager has a friendship meter measured in **hearts** (up to 10). The more hearts, the friendlier they are.

- **0–2 hearts:** Acquaintance. They barely know you.
- **3–7 hearts:** Friends. You'll start receiving recipes and letters.
- **8 hearts:** Close friends. For marriageable NPCs, this is the max until you start dating.
- **10 hearts:** Best friends (or dating, for romanceable NPCs).
- **Marriage:** Your spouse can go all the way to **14 hearts**.

## How to gain friendship

### 1. Talk to everyone, every day

Simply speaking to a villager gives **+20 friendship points** once per day. It's small, but it adds up — and it *prevents* friendship decay.

### 2. Give gifts (the big one)

Gifts are the fastest way to build friendship. You can give:

- **Two gifts per week** to each villager (resets Sunday).
- **One extra gift on their birthday** — worth **8x** the normal amount.

Loved gifts give the most points; hated gifts *lower* friendship. See our [gift guide](/guides/best-gifts-villagers/) for a full list.

### 3. Complete quests

Help Wanted board requests, story quests, and bulletin board bundles all boost friendship with the villager involved.

### 4. Attend festivals

Some festivals (like the Luau, which involves a community potluck) can raise friendship with everyone at once.

## How friendship decays

Friendship slowly **decays** if you ignore someone — usually by a small amount each day you don't interact. The key rules:

- **Talking resets the decay** for that day.
- **Maxed-out hearts (8 or 10) stop decaying** for non-romanceable villagers.
- Your **spouse's hearts decay faster** if neglected, so keep chatting.

## Heart events

At certain heart levels, entering a location at the right time triggers a **heart event** — a cutscene that reveals more about the character. These are worth seeking out:

- They give you story and character depth.
- Some have choices that affect future friendship.
- They're required to date (8-heart events) and marry (10-heart events) a romanceable NPC.

## How to earn hearts efficiently

1. **Do a daily "gift run."** Carry a stack of a universal love (like gems) and hand them out as you pass people.
2. **Never miss a birthday.** A loved birthday gift is the single biggest friendship boost in the game.
3. **Talk as you walk.** Greet everyone you pass — it costs nothing and stops decay.
4. **Prioritize the villagers you want recipes or romance from.**

## The bottom line

Friendship is a slow-and-steady system: **talk daily, give loved gifts twice a week, and never miss a birthday.** Do that consistently and you'll unlock recipes, cutscenes, and even marriage far faster than you expect.
