An iridium Truffle can be worth more than the oil made from it. Before building a row of Oil Makers, check whether the character selling the goods has Artisan and compare the two actual sale prices.

## Two comparisons that change the answer

| Sale | Without Artisan | With Artisan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iridium Truffle | 1,250g | 1,250g |
| Truffle Oil | 1,065g | 1,491g |
| Oil minus raw sale | −185g | +241g |

These figures use the [Truffle](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Truffle) and [Truffle Oil](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Truffle_Oil) base-game values. The raw item is forage, so Rancher does not increase its price. Botanist can make collected truffles iridium quality; Artisan improves the processed oil instead.

For ten iridium truffles, processing without Artisan reduces sales by 1,850g. With Artisan, it adds 2,410g. That is a calculation for this particular input quality, not a claim that oil always wins or always loses.

## Keep a reason for each Oil Maker

One truffle takes six in-game hours to become oil. A player who visits the processing area once daily should budget around that reliable collection round, rather than assuming every machine will run at its theoretical maximum.

If you produce more truffles than you want to reload, sort the queue. Process the inputs with the most worthwhile margin and sell the rest. You do not need enough machines to handle the largest lucky day immediately. A small buffer chest is cheaper than an oversized workshop.

You may also need oil for a bundle or crafting even when raw sales pay more. Put those units aside first. A required ingredient and a sale item have different purposes; the price table should not accidentally block progression.

## The pigs still need outdoor space

Oil production starts with collecting truffles. Pigs need suitable outdoor conditions, and Winter is not an ordinary production season for them. Uncollected truffles disappear at the season change. An [Auto-Grabber](/guides/auto-grabber-auto-petter-difference/) will not do this outdoor collection for you.

Keep the area around the barn readable and walk it before ending the day. A decorative corner is fine, but make sure you can see and reach items around it. Use the [animal guide](/guides/animals-guide/) when planning expansion, and reserve [Winter feeding supplies](/guides/silo-hay-winter-feeding-guide/) before spending all of the autumn income on more pigs.
