Hand-watering crops is the biggest time sink in Stardew Valley. Sprinklers automate it entirely — and the moment you set them up, you reclaim hours every single day. Here's how to use them.

## The three types of sprinklers

| Sprinkler | Waters | Recipe (bars + extras) |
|-----------|--------|------------------------|
| ![Sprinkler](/images/items/sprinkler.png) Sprinkler | 4 tiles (up/down/left/right) | 1 ![Copper Bar](/images/items/copper-bar.png) Copper + 1 ![Iron Bar](/images/items/iron-bar.png) Iron |
| ![Quality Sprinkler](/images/items/quality-sprinkler.png) Quality Sprinkler | 8 tiles (surrounding) | 1 ![Iron Bar](/images/items/iron-bar.png) Iron + 1 ![Gold Bar](/images/items/gold-bar.png) Gold + 1 Refined Quartz |
| ![Iridium Sprinkler](/images/items/iridium-sprinkler.png) Iridium Sprinkler | 24 tiles (5x5 minus corners) | 1 ![Iridium Bar](/images/items/iridium-bar.png) Iridium + 1 ![Gold Bar](/images/items/gold-bar.png) Gold + 1 Battery Pack |

You unlock these recipes by leveling the **Farming** skill (and the Iridium Sprinkler is also sold by Krobus and the Traveling Cart).

## Which sprinkler should you use?

- **Skip the basic Sprinkler.** It only waters 4 tiles and isn't worth the materials for most layouts.
- **Rush Quality Sprinklers.** They water a clean 3x3 area and are the workhorse of the mid-game. The hard part is getting Refined Quartz (smelt Quartz) and Gold bars.
- **Iridium Sprinklers** are the endgame — huge coverage, and you can buy them from Krobus (every Friday) once you reach the Sewers.

> To make Quality Sprinklers early, prioritize **mining for iron and gold** and smelt ![Quartz](/images/items/quartz.png) Quartz into Refined Quartz. A few trips to the mines and you can automate your whole farm.

## Sprinkler layouts

### Quality Sprinkler grid

Place Quality Sprinklers in a grid where each covers its surrounding 8 tiles. A common pattern spaces them so every crop tile is watered with no gaps.

### Iridium Sprinkler grid

Iridium Sprinklers cover 24 tiles (a 5x5 square minus the four corners). Space them in rows for full coverage.

## Tips for efficient watering

- **Place sprinklers first, then plant around them.** It's easier than retrofitting.
- **Scarecrows matter.** Sprinklers don't protect crops — keep scarecrows nearby.
- **Upgrade your hoe** to till large fields fast when you set up new sprinkler grids.
- **Greenhouse and Ginger Island** benefit enormously from sprinklers too.

## The bottom line

Automating watering is one of the best investments in the game. **Skip the basic sprinkler, rush Quality Sprinklers with mined iron and gold, and eventually switch to Iridium Sprinklers.** Once your farm waters itself, you'll wonder how you ever played without them.
