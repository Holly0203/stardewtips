Spring is where every farm starts, and the seeds you choose in those first 28 days set the tone for your entire first year. This guide breaks down every Spring crop by profit so you can spend your starting gold wisely.

## The golden rule: profit per day

When comparing crops, never look at the sale price alone. A crop that sells for a lot but takes forever to grow might earn you less per day than a cheap, fast crop you can replant over and over.

The formula is simple:

```
profit per day = (sell price - seed cost) / days to grow
```

Use this to compare everything below.

## What to plant in Year 1

### Week 1: Parsnips and Potatoes

Your first 15 parsnip seeds come free. Plant them immediately. Once you have some gold, Potatoes are your best early bet because they grow fast and have a chance to yield extra potatoes per harvest.

### Mid-spring: Kale

Kale is the sleeper pick of Spring. It's cheap, grows in just 6 days, and earns a solid profit per day. Many min-max players fill their farm with Kale after the first Potato harvest.

### The Strawberry rush

On the 13th of Spring, the Egg Festival sells Strawberry seeds — the only time you can buy them. If you can afford them, buy as many as possible. Plant them immediately and they'll keep producing fruit every 4 days until the end of the season.

> Save some gold before the Egg Festival. Strawberry seeds cost 100g each, and they're worth every coin.

## Tips to maximize Spring income

- **Water every day.** Crops don't grow on days you skip watering.
- **Upgrade your watering can** when you can afford it, or focus on crops with fast cycles.
- **Watch the calendar.** Every crop dies when the season changes, so don't plant anything with a grow time longer than the days remaining.
- **Reinvest early.** The more seeds you can buy now, the more you harvest later.

## The bottom line

For pure profit per day in Year 1 Spring, **Kale and Potatoes are your workhorses**, while **Strawberries are your big payoff** if you plan ahead for the Egg Festival. Start with Parsnips, roll into Potatoes and Kale, and save up for the Strawberry rush.
