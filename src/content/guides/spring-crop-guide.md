Spring is where every farm starts, and the seeds you choose in those first 28 days set the tone for your entire year. This guide ranks every Spring crop by real profit numbers and gives you a week-by-week plan that works whether you're playing Year 1 or replaying the season with saved seeds.

> **At a glance**
>
> - **Best starter crop:** ![Parsnip](/images/items/parsnip.png) Parsnips — you get 15 free seeds on Day 1.
> - **Best early money:** ![Potato](/images/items/potato.png) Potatoes (extra yield chance) and Lettuce (~11g/day profit per hill).
> - **Best single investment:** ![Strawberry](/images/items/strawberry.png) Strawberries from the Egg Festival on Spring 13.
> - **Don't plant long crops** (Cauliflower, Rhubarb) after Spring 16 — they won't finish before Summer.
> - Every Spring crop dies on Summer 1, so plan your last planting dates carefully.

## The golden rule: profit per day

Never judge a crop by its sale price alone. A 175g Cauliflower that takes 12 days can earn you *less* per day than a cheap crop you can replant three times. Compare everything with one formula:

```
profit per day = (sell price − seed cost) ÷ days to grow
```

For regrowing crops, count all the harvests you'll actually get before Spring ends instead of just one.

## What to plant in Year 1

### Days 1–4: free Parsnips, then Potatoes

Your backpack starts with 15 ![Parsnip Seeds](/images/items/parsnip-seeds.png) — plant and water them on Day 1. They're ready by Day 5 and fund your first real seed purchases. Alongside them, ![Potato Seeds](/images/items/potato-seeds.png) are the smartest early spend: 6 days, 80g each, and every harvest has a chance to yield extra potatoes, which pushes their real average above 100g per hill.

If you're sitting on a few hundred gold by mid-month, cheap Lettuce Seeds quietly out-earn Kale per day. Kale is still the better *total* crop if you plan to ship in bulk — pick either and you can't go far wrong.

### Mid-spring: the Strawberry rush

On **Spring 13 the Egg Festival** sells ![Strawberry Seeds](/images/items/strawberry-seeds.png) for 100g — the only day and place you can buy them. Strawberries take 8 days, then regrow every 4 days, so seeds planted on the 13rd give you two harvests (Spring 21 and 25) worth 240g — a **~140g net profit per seed**, more than double what Kale returns over the same period.

The real trick comes in Year 2 and beyond: every strawberry you harvest can be turned back into seeds at the seed maker. Save a batch in your chest before Spring ends, plant them on **Spring 1**, and a single strawberry hill returns roughly 5 harvests (around 600g). That's about **20g per day per plant** — nothing else in Spring comes close.

### Late-season specials: Rhubarb and Cauliflower

**Rhubarb** (100g seed, 13 days, 220g) is Spring's high-roller, but its seeds are only sold at the **Oasis in Calico Desert**, which means you need the bus repaired first — realistically a Year 2 goal. Buy a few on Spring 1–15 and bank 9g/day per hill.

**Cauliflower** (12 days, 175g) is slower per day but can merge into a **Giant Cauliflower** if you plant it in a 3×3 block — a giant harvest drops several big cauliflower at once and is worth the gamble if you have spare space. Full mechanics are in the [Community Center guide](/guides/community-center-guide/) (the Pantry's Spring Crops bundle asks for one) and our [farm layout tips](/guides/farm-layout-tips/) cover where to place 3×3 blocks without wasting sprinkler coverage.

## Spring bundles and the Community Center

The Community Center's **Spring Crops bundle** (in the Pantry) wants one each of Parsnip, Green Bean, Cauliflower, and Potato — about 335g of shipped value, paid back as **20 Speed-Gro** fertilizer. Don't ship your first of everything; hold it for the shrine. The [full bundle breakdown](/guides/community-center-guide/) lists every requirement and reward.

## Tips to maximize Spring income

- **Water every day.** A missed day doesn't kill a crop, it delays it — which can push it past the season's end. Rainy days water everything for free.
- **Watch the calendar.** Crops die when the season flips. Cauliflower and Rhubarb (13 days) must be planted by Spring 15 at the latest; anything longer simply won't pay out.
- **Craft sprinklers before you buy luxuries.** The Sprinkler recipe unlocks at Farming level 2 and pays for itself the first week — see the [sprinkler guide](/guides/sprinkler-guide/).
- **Reinvest aggressively.** Spring profit exists to fund Strawberry seeds and Summer Blueberries — the [Year 1 money guide](/guides/make-money-year-one/) shows the full cash-flow plan.

## The bottom line

For raw profit per day in Year 1, **Potatoes and Lettuce are your workhorses, Strawberries are your jackpot, and Rhubarb is the stretch goal** once the desert opens. Start with free Parsnips, roll into Potatoes, keep 1,000g+ for the Egg Festival on the 13th, and turn every harvest back into seeds.
