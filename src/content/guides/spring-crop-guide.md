Keep the first field small enough to water comfortably. Parsnips give a quick first harvest; Potatoes and Kale are options for reinvesting it. Set aside money for the Egg Festival if you want Strawberries, and check the last harvest date before buying a slow crop.

## Compare the seed bill with the harvest

For a single-harvest crop, subtract the seed cost from the expected sale value. Then consider the growing time and the chance to replant before the season ends. A regrowing crop needs a count of the harvests remaining, not just the value of its first pick.

The table uses normal-quality base prices without professions or growth bonuses. Extra yields can change the result.

| Crop | Seed cost | Base sale value | Initial growth | Later harvests |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Parsnip | 20g | 35g | 4 days | Replant |
| Potato | 50g | 80g per potato | 6 days | Replant; extra yield possible |
| Kale | 70g | 110g | 6 days | Replant |
| Cauliflower | 80g | 175g | 12 days | Replant |
| Green Bean | 60g | 40g | 10 days | Every 3 days |
| Strawberry | 100g at Egg Festival | 120g | 8 days | Every 4 days |
| Rhubarb | 100g at Oasis | 220g | 13 days | Replant |

See the [crop reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Crops) for individual entries and modifiers.

## The first few plantings

On the Standard Farm, plant the starter Parsnips and keep the patch manageable. Their quick harvest lets you try the planting and watering routine before taking on more tiles. Other farm maps can have different starting gifts.

For the next purchase, choose enough seeds to use your current field rather than clearing a much larger area because the packets are affordable. Watering time becomes the constraint well before the map runs out of space.

Try a few different crops if bundles are a goal. The standard Spring Crops bundle requires a Parsnip, Green Bean, Cauliflower, and Potato. Check the [Community Center guide](/guides/community-center-guide/) and keep the required items before shipping the rest.

## Set a Strawberry budget

The Egg Festival on Spring 13 sells Strawberry Seeds for 100g each. Ten packets cost 1,000g; twenty cost 2,000g. Choose the field size before visiting, and keep money for anything else the farm needs.

Without growth bonuses, daily-watered seeds planted on Spring 13 produce on Spring 21 and 25. That is two planned harvests before the season ends. The [festival planting plan](/guides/egg-festival-strawberry-plan/) shows the assumptions and the simple return calculation.

Seeds reserved for next year are a separate investment from seeds planted now. If you use a [Seed Maker](/guides/seed-maker-guide/), count the actual output and compare the value of the sacrificed fruit with buying packets.

## Watch late plantings

A crop with thirteen days of growth needs planting by Spring 15 to finish on Spring 28 without speed bonuses. A twelve-day crop can be planted by Spring 16 under the same conditions. Missing water delays the harvest, so the last possible date leaves no margin.

Rhubarb also requires access to its seed source at the Oasis. It is an option if the bus is already available, not a necessary target for every new farm.

If there is too little time for another planting, use the field's remaining days for something else. Fishing or a short mine trip can be more useful than purchasing seeds that cannot finish.

## Prepare for Summer without overspending

Keep a little cash available on the last days of Spring, check your planned field size, and collect any crops due before the change. Most Spring crops do not survive into Summer.

Before adding another large seed bill, see whether [sprinklers](/guides/sprinkler-guide/) can reduce the watering load. Then use the [Summer crop plan](/guides/summer-crop-guide/) to choose the next field. You do not need to replant every tile on the first morning for the new season to work.

## A planting check before spending the next 500g

Ten Potato Seeds cost **500g** and create ten watered crop positions for six growing days. At one normal Potato per plant, the harvest baseline is **800g gross**, or **300g above seed cost**, before extra yield and modifiers.

If the same 500g was reserved for food or an upcoming payment, the crop does not repay it until harvest and sale. Keep the spending date visible. For a bundle-focused patch, one Green Bean, one Cauliflower, one Potato, and one Parsnip serve a different purpose from an all-Potato income block; assign those reserves before shipping.

**References:** values and seasonal rules checked against the linked Crops reference on 24 October 2026; Strawberry dates use the [Strawberry growth rules](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Strawberry). Planning assumes normal-quality output, no growth bonuses, daily watering, and no crop loss.
