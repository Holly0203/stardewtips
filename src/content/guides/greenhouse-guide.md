The Greenhouse is Stardew Valley's best late-first-year reward: a fully tillable field where **every season counts as the current one** — no frost, no dying crops, no waiting for Spring. Unlock it, fill it with the right perennials, and your income stops being seasonal altogether. Here's exactly how to get it and what to plant.

> **At a glance**
>
> - The Greenhouse is unlocked by completing the **Pantry** room in the Community Center — not the Crafts Room, which is the most repeated rumor in the game.
> - Joja route alternative: buy the Greenhouse project for **35,000g**.
> - **120 tillable soil tiles** (a 12×10 patch) that never leave season.
> - You still have to **water** — rain doesn't come indoors. Sprinklers fix that.
> - End goal: a full field of ![Ancient Fruit](/images/items/ancient-fruit.png) Ancient Fruit feeding a keg lineup.

## How to unlock the Greenhouse

Community Center route: finish all **six Pantry bundles** — the four crop bundles (Spring, Summer, Fall, and Quality Crops) plus Animal and Artisan. When the Pantry shrine completes, a cutscene plays and the smashed greenhouse on the east side of your farm is restored.

Don't confuse the rooms: in v1.6 the **foraging** bundles live in the **Crafts Room** (its reward is the bridge to the Quarry), while crops are the Pantry's job — and the Keg comes from the Pantry's own Artisan bundle. The full bundle-by-bundle list, with the exact items to save, is in our [Community Center guide](/guides/community-center-guide/).

If you took the JojaMart membership instead, the Greenhouse is a 35,000g project on the development board.

## What makes the Greenhouse different

- **120 tillable tiles in a 12×10 grid**, plus tillable ground around the edges — enough for serious tree and sprinkler layouts.
- **Seasons don't apply.** A Fall-only crop like Cranberries grows in January; a Spring crop is fine in Summer.
- **Crops never die at season change.** Perennials run forever; annuals can be replanted the same day.
- **No crows and no lightning** — you can skip the scarecrow entirely.
- It does **not** come with free water: you'll refill your can from the pond in the pond area next to the shipping bin, or simply install sprinklers like everywhere else.
- Fruit trees planted inside grow and bear fruit **year-round** (regular timber trees don't). Bee houses and giant-crop merging don't work inside — the ceiling says no.

## The best Greenhouse crops

### Ancient Fruit — the long game winner

Donate the ![Ancient Seed](/images/items/ancient-seeds.png) Ancient Seed artifact to the Museum and you get the plantable seed. It takes 28 days to ripen, then produces fruit **every 7 days forever**, selling at 550g a piece — or **1,650g as Ancient Fruit Wine** from a Keg. Nothing else in the game combines zero replanting with wine-tier value. A full Greenhouse of Ancient Fruit is the classic "retirement plan" layout: plant once, refill the kegs weekly, done.

### Starfruit — the high-turn cash crop

![Starfruit](/images/items/starfruit.png) seeds cost 400g from the Oasis, mature in 13 days, and sell at 750g (2,250g as wine). Inside the Greenhouse there's no season deadline, so replanting is trivial. Starfruit wins in short bursts; Ancient Fruit wins over years — many players run both.

### Sweet Gem Berry — the season-proof jackpot

Outside, the 24-day ![Sweet Gem Berry](/images/items/sweet-gem-berry.png) needs a Rare Seed planted by Fall 4 or it dies unharvested. Inside, you can plant a 1,000g seed on any day of the year and ship 3,000g, every 24 days, forever. With sprinklers and no seasonal stress, this is the purest profit-per-day the Greenhouse can produce — just remember the Traveling Cart is the only seed source (the [fall crop guide](/guides/fall-crop-guide/) covers the outdoor math).

### Coffee and Hops — the early fillers

While you're still gathering Ancient Seeds: **Coffee Bean** plants (from the Traveling Cart) regrow every 2 days and the drink itself sells well, and **Hops** regrow daily for Pale Ale kegs. Both are good "first Greenhouse" plantings.

## A recommended layout

- **Sprinklers:** ![Iridium Sprinkler](/images/items/iridium-sprinkler.png) six iridium sprinklers cover the whole 12×10 soil plot, and they can sit **on the border tiles** so they cost you zero planting space — the trick that makes the Greenhouse the most efficient field in the game. The [sprinkler guide](/guides/sprinkler-guide/) has the coverage math.
- **Trees around the outside soil:** fruit trees on the border strip give daily fruit with no maintenance; 18-tree layouts are common.
- **Interior:** Ancient Fruit or Sweet Gem Berry rows, with a side plot for your replanting rotation.

## The bottom line

Unlock the Greenhouse by finishing the **Pantry** (or 35,000g at Joja), cover the 120 tiles with six border iridium sprinklers, and fill it with Ancient Fruit for permanent wine income — Starfruit and Sweet Gem Berries for the impatient. Once it's running, Winter stops being an off-season; see the [winter guide](/guides/winter-guide/) for what to do while the outdoor farm sleeps. One hover per tip over in the [Tip Galaxy](/).
