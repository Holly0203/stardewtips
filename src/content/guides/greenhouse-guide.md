The Greenhouse lets crops keep growing through season changes. You still need to water them and make room for harvesting. Start with crops and sprinklers you already have, then replace temporary plantings as your seed supply improves.

> **At a glance**
>
> - The Greenhouse is unlocked by completing the **Pantry** room in the Community Center.
> - Joja route alternative: buy the Greenhouse project for **35,000g**.
> - **120 tillable soil tiles** (a 12×10 patch) that never leave season.
> - You still have to **water** — rain doesn't come indoors. Sprinklers fix that.
> - End goal: a full field of ![Ancient Fruit](/images/items/ancient-fruit.png) Ancient Fruit feeding a keg lineup.

## How to unlock the Greenhouse

Community Center route: finish all **six Pantry bundles** — the four crop bundles (Spring, Summer, Fall, and Quality Crops) plus Animal and Artisan. When the Pantry shrine completes, a cutscene plays and the smashed greenhouse on the east side of your farm is restored.

Don't confuse the rooms: in v1.6 the **foraging** bundles live in the **Crafts Room** (its reward is the bridge to the Quarry), while crops are the Pantry's job — and the Keg comes from the Pantry's own Artisan bundle. The room-planning explanation is in our [Community Center guide](/guides/community-center-guide/).

If you took the JojaMart membership instead, the Greenhouse is a 35,000g project on the development board.

## What makes the Greenhouse different

- **120 crop tiles in a 12×10 grid**, with a separate border area that can accommodate fruit trees.
- **Seasons don't apply.** A Fall-only crop like Cranberries grows in Winter; a Spring crop is fine in Summer.
- **Crops never die at season change.** Perennials run forever; annuals can be replanted the same day.
- **No crows and no lightning** — you can skip the scarecrow entirely.
- It does **not** come with free water: you'll refill your can at the trough along the north wall, or simply install sprinklers like everywhere else.
- Fruit trees planted inside grow and bear fruit **year-round** (regular timber trees don't). Bee houses and giant-crop merging don't work inside .

## Choosing the crop mix

### Ancient Fruit for repeated harvests

Donate the ![Ancient Seed](/images/items/ancient-seeds.png) Ancient Seed artifact to the Museum and you get the plantable seed. It takes 28 days to ripen, then produces fruit **every 7 days forever**, selling at 550g a piece — or **1,650g as Ancient Fruit Wine** from a Keg. A repeated harvest avoids buying another packet after every pick. Build the seed supply and keg capacity gradually; you can use the remaining tiles for temporary crops.

### Starfruit — the high-turn cash crop

![Starfruit](/images/items/starfruit.png) seeds cost 400g from the Oasis, mature in 13 days, and sell at 750g (2,250g as wine). There is no outdoor season deadline inside, but each harvest still needs replanting money. Compare that cost with the available keg capacity.

### Sweet Gem Berry when seeds are available

Outside, the 24-day ![Sweet Gem Berry](/images/items/sweet-gem-berry.png) needs a Rare Seed planted by Fall 4 or it dies unharvested. Inside, the crop has no Fall deadline, but every planting needs another seed. That replacement supply and the long growth period matter when comparing it with regrowing crops. The [Fall crop guide](/guides/fall-crop-guide/) explains the basic purchase and harvest calculation.

### Coffee and Hops — the early fillers

While you're still gathering Ancient Seeds: **Coffee Bean** plants (from the Traveling Cart) regrow every 2 days and the drink itself sells well, and **Hops** regrow daily for Pale Ale kegs. Both are good "first Greenhouse" plantings.

## A recommended layout

- **Sprinklers:** ![Iridium Sprinkler](/images/items/iridium-sprinkler.png) a standard six-unit Iridium layout covers the plot with four units on the growing area and two on the border, leaving 116 crop tiles without range attachments. The [sprinkler guide](/guides/sprinkler-guide/) has the coverage math.
- **Trees around the outside soil:** fruit trees on the border strip give daily fruit with no maintenance; 18-tree layouts are common.
- **Interior:** Ancient Fruit or Sweet Gem Berry rows, with a side plot for your replanting rotation.

## Before planting the whole plot

Place the sprinklers, inspect their coverage, and leave access to every crop. Start with the seeds available rather than leaving useful tiles empty while waiting for a preferred crop. Check the [fruit tree guide](/guides/fruit-tree-planting-guide/) before fitting saplings around the edge.
