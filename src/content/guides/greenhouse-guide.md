The Greenhouse is one of the best rewards in Stardew Valley: a field you can grow crops in **all year round**, immune to seasons. Here's how to unlock it and what to plant.

## How to unlock the Greenhouse

The Greenhouse is the reward for completing the **Pantry** in the Community Center. You'll need to complete all its bundles:

- Spring Crops, Summer Crops, Fall Crops, Quality Crops
- Animal Bundle and Artisan Bundle

Alternatively, if you went the Joja route, you can simply **pay Joja to restore it**.

> Most players find the Community Center route worth it — the Pantry bundles are straightforward if you plant a variety of crops each season.

## What makes the Greenhouse special

- **120 tillable tiles** that work in every season.
- **Crops never die** from a season change.
- **Fruit trees** can be planted around the inner edge and produce fruit year-round.
- A **water trough** refills your watering can — no trip to the farm pond.

## The best crops to grow

### Ancient Fruit — the long-term king

![Ancient Fruit](/images/items/ancient-fruit.png) Ancient Fruit is the best long-term crop in the game:

- It **keeps producing fruit every 7 days** forever.
- Turn it into **wine** in a Keg for massive profit.
- Get your first seed from the ![Ancient Seed](/images/items/ancient-seed.png) Ancient Seed artifact (donate to the museum to get the plantable seed).

Once your Greenhouse is full of Ancient Fruit, you have a permanent, high-value income stream.

### Starfruit — the high-value cash crop

![Starfruit](/images/items/starfruit.png) Starfruit is the most valuable single harvest. Buy seeds from the Oasis, plant, harvest, and keg into wine. It's the best raw moneymaker if you don't mind replanting.

### Coffee Beans — the early filler

![Coffee Bean](/images/items/coffee-bean.png) Coffee Beans grow into coffee plants that regrow every 2 days. They're a great way to fill the Greenhouse early while you build up Ancient Seeds, and the coffee itself sells well and gives a speed buff.

### Fruit trees

Plant **fruit trees** around the edge of the Greenhouse (in the tiled border area). They produce fruit every day regardless of season — a nice passive bonus.

## A recommended layout

- **Sprinklers:** Place ![Iridium Sprinkler](/images/items/iridium-sprinkler.png) Iridium Sprinklers to cover the field automatically. A common layout fits 6 iridium sprinklers on the edges, leaving the middle for more.
- **Fruit trees** around the perimeter.
- **Fill the rest with Ancient Fruit** (or Starfruit/coffee while you build up seeds).

## The bottom line

The Greenhouse is your ticket to **year-round, season-proof income**. Unlock it as fast as you can, plant Ancient Fruit for the long game (or Starfruit for quick cash), and let sprinklers do the watering. A full Greenhouse is one of the best money setups in the game.
