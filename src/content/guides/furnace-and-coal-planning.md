If every furnace is empty because coal ran out, another furnace will not shorten the job. Count the ore and fuel for the next recipe first. Extra machines help when the queue is waiting on processing time, not when every machine is waiting on the same ingredient.

## Start from the finished recipe

A normal metal bar requires **five matching ore and one Coal** in a standard Furnace. The furnace itself needs **20 Copper Ore and 25 Stone** to craft. Clint provides the blueprint after you acquire copper and see the relevant morning cutscene.

These rules are documented in the [Furnace reference](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Furnace). Separate ore reserved for building another furnace from ore intended to become bars; the same stack cannot do both jobs.

For a tool upgrade needing five bars, the smelting component is **twenty-five ore and five Coal**, in addition to Clint's payment. Our [tool upgrade guide](/guides/tool-upgrades-guide/) explains the hand-in and collection timing after the bars are ready.

## Work through a mixed-machine batch

Suppose you want four machines, each needing one Iron Bar and one Gold Bar. The smelting list is:

| Resource | Needed for eight bars |
|---|---:|
| Iron Ore | 20 |
| Gold Ore | 20 |
| Coal | 8 |

That excludes coal required directly by the finished machines' own recipes. Read both the bar inputs and the final crafting recipe before declaring the batch funded.

A reserve labeled only “ore for equipment” is easy to overestimate. A written count for the actual next batch makes it clear whether the missing material is iron, gold, or fuel.

## Match the cycle to your time on the farm

Copper smelts in **thirty minutes**, Iron in **two hours**, Gold in **five hours**, and Iridium in **eight hours**. These are in-game processing durations, with overnight clock behavior affecting how the wait feels across a day.

When several metals are needed, choose an order that fits your route. Short jobs can run while you finish nearby chores; a longer batch can be loaded before leaving. You do not need to stand beside a furnace, but the next batch will wait if you never return to collect the first.

A group of furnaces is most useful when you have complete inputs ready for every unit. If you can only load one, three empty extras are not improving today's output.

## Give coal competing jobs a budget

Coal may also be going into [Fish Smokers](/guides/fish-smoker-coal-profit/), jars, or other recipes. Before processing a low-value catch or crafting an optional machine, check the amount needed for the next essential bar batch.

One simple reserve policy is to keep the next upgrade's fuel untouched, then allocate the rest to ongoing processing. Adjust that policy when the upgrade is finished instead of keeping a permanent pile with no named use.

If gathering coal is the real constraint, use a targeted [mine outing](/guides/mining-guide-beginners/) or compare accessible purchase and production options. Keep that decision separate from buying additional processing capacity.

## Quartz needs its own input count

A normal Quartz plus Coal becomes one Refined Quartz. Fire Quartz plus Coal becomes three Refined Quartz in the same standard system. That difference matters when planning an equipment batch.

Check whether a new mineral is reserved for donation or another recipe before smelting it. If your fishing chest contains Broken Glasses or Broken CDs, a [Recycling Machine](/guides/recycling-machine-guide/) provides another route to Refined Quartz without assigning furnace fuel to that operation.

Do not group all purple-looking or crystal-like items into a generic “quartz” stack. The recipe needs the actual supported input.

## When another furnace earns its place

Add capacity if the full next batch is funded and the delay is preventing a project you want to do soon. Before crafting, check where you will collect it and whether you can access every unit from the route.

If bars are already accumulating faster than you use them, finish the planned equipment rather than expanding the workshop. If the queue remains short, one or two well-used furnaces can be enough for the current stage.

**Reference:** inputs, recipe, metal durations, and quartz conversions checked against the linked Furnace Wiki entry on 24 October 2026. Batch lists use ordinary furnaces; Heavy Furnace rules are different and are not included.
