The Island Farmhouse makes an island overnight possible; it does not take care of the animals you left behind. Before spending several days on Ginger Island, arrange both farms around how often you will actually visit.

## What the farmhouse purchase includes

After gaining access to the western area, repairing the house costs 20 Golden Walnuts. It provides beds, a kitchen, and a shipping bin. A mailbox costs another five walnuts; the return Farm Obelisk costs 20 after that upgrade. The [Island Farmhouse reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Island_Farmhouse) lists this sequence.

The house, mailbox, and return obelisk total 45 walnuts, excluding earlier access costs. If you only have enough for the house, plan your journey home separately rather than assuming a return obelisk is included.

## Plant for the interval between visits

The [island farm](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Ginger_Island) allows crops regardless of the mainland season, and crows do not require scarecrows there. Watering still matters. Install coverage before filling a large field, and test that every planted edge is reached.

Begin with a small block. Harvest it on your next planned visit, check whether the timing fits, then expand. An all-season farm removes a seasonal restriction; it does not remove the work of planting, collecting, and transporting produce.

Keep seeds, a few essential tools, and replacement supplies on the island. A chest there prevents minor shortages from forcing a return trip. Mark reserves clearly so you do not ship your next planting with the sale stock.

## Mainland care needs a real arrangement

Review every animal building before staying away. Hay must be available, and the feeding arrangement must match the building's upgrades. A full silo alone does not place food onto every feeding bench. See the [Winter feeding guide](/guides/silo-hay-winter-feeding-guide/) for the same indoor-care principles.

An [Auto-Grabber or Auto-Petter](/guides/auto-grabber-auto-petter-difference/) can reduce specific chores, but neither supplies feed. On the crop side, sprinklers cover watering while harvest timing may still require a mainland visit.

For a first overnight, finish mainland chores, travel, and use the island evening for preparation rather than beginning an ambitious dungeon run late. Starting the next morning nearby gives you more usable time for the [Volcano route](/guides/volcano-dungeon-first-forge-route/).

After returning home, check what accumulated. A full collector, missed harvest, or shortage tells you which part of the arrangement needs changing before a longer stay. Expand the island operation only as quickly as your two-farm schedule remains manageable.

## Pack a first overnight deliberately

Before boarding, cover mainland feeding, check watering coverage, and collect any harvest that would delay regrowth while you are away. Take the supplies for the island job and one usable return option. Leave unrelated gifts and construction stacks at home.

On arrival, verify the repaired house and bed before the evening becomes a dungeon trip. Store spare seed and recovery supplies there so the next visit starts from a prepared base. If returning early is necessary, do it before spending the last daylight on another unlock; the mainland animals do not receive food simply because the island house has been repaired.
