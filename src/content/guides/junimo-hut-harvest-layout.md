A Junimo Hut saves harvest clicks, but a field designed around its roof can leave the edges outside its range. Measure from the doorway and keep a manual route through the crops for days when the helpers stay inside.

## Mark the working square first

The harvest area is a 17-by-17 square centered on the doorway: eight tiles out in each direction. The building occupies a 3-by-2 footprint within that plan. Unlocking the building requires the Wizard's postgame quest progression; construction costs 20,000g, 200 Stone, nine Starfruit, and 100 Fiber. See the [Junimo Hut reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Junimo_Hut) for the building rules.

Before buying, use paths or empty tilled ground to mark the boundary. Lay your sprinkler coverage over that square next. Trying to force a familiar sprinkler block around an already placed hut can leave narrow strips that are watered but never harvested.

Keep the doorway clear and leave yourself an aisle. Junimos can cross trellis crops, but you still need to water, replant, and collect manually when required. The [farm layout guide](/guides/farm-layout-tips/) helps separate walking space from planted space.

## Rain changes the job

Junimos do not harvest in rain or Winter. They also do not gather tree fruit, Garden Pot crops, or Wild Seed forage crops. Their harvesting gives you no Farming experience. These limits matter more than squeezing a few extra plants into a corner.

For a single-harvest crop, a rain delay may only postpone cash. For a regrowing crop, delaying collection also delays the next growth cycle. Check the forecast when a harvest date approaches, and decide whether a manual pass is worth the saved day.

## Why a crop is still standing

Check range first, then weather, the harvesting toggle, and storage. The hut's bag holds 36 stacks; empty it before a large mixed harvest. Three helpers need time to collect plants, so inspect the field later rather than assuming an unfinished morning means the layout is broken.

Keep a small replanting chest near the field entrance. A Junimo harvest does not put the next seed in the ground. Reserve seed money before shipping everything, especially when planting [expensive seasonal crops](/guides/summer-crop-guide/).

Build one working block before copying it across the farm. Watch one harvest, walk the rainy-day route yourself, and only then repeat the pattern. [Sprinkler coverage](/guides/sprinkler-guide/) and harvesting coverage are separate checks.
