The mines are where you'll find the ores and gems that fund your tool upgrades, feed the Community Center, and pad your wallet. This guide walks you through the whole thing, from the first rock to the bottom.

## Getting into the mines

The mines are in the mountains, northeast of town. The entrance is blocked by a rockslide until **Spring 5**, when it's cleared automatically. Head inside and you'll find **120 floors** to descend.

## How the floors work

- There are **120 levels**, going deeper each time.
- An **elevator activates every 5 floors**, letting you fast-travel back to any checkpoint.
- Each floor has rocks to break and enemies to fight; find a **ladder** to descend.
- Some floors are "infested" with enemies (kill them all to reveal the ladder), and some have **treasure chests**.

## Combat basics

You'll fight **Slimes, Bats, Rock Crabs, and more** as you descend. A few tips:

- **Swing your sword** as enemies approach — timing matters.
- **Watch your health bar** and eat food to heal before it gets low.
- **Upgrade your weapon** whenever you find a better one (in chests or from the Adventurer's Guild).
- **Bring food** — mining drains energy, and combat drains health.

## What to bring

- A **pickaxe** (upgrade it to break rocks faster).
- A **weapon** (sword or club).
- **Food** for energy and healing.
- **Space in your backpack** — you'll fill it fast.

## Why mining matters

- **Ores** fund tool upgrades and crafting (sprinklers, kegs, and more).
- **Gems** are great money and gifts.
- **Geodes** (cracked open by Clint for 25g each) contain minerals and artifacts for the museum.
- **Combat levels** unlock new recipes and gear.

## The bottom line

Mine in **5-floor chunks** — descend until you reach the next elevator, then head home before you run out of energy or health. Prioritize ![Iron Ore](/images/items/iron-ore.png) iron for tool upgrades and quality sprinklers, keep the gems you need for bundles and gifts, and you'll be sitting on a fortune by the time you hit floor 120.
