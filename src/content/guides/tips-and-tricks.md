Stardew Valley is packed with small details that make a big difference. Here are 25 tips to play smarter.

## Farming

1. **Compare profit per day, not sell price.** A cheap fast crop can beat an expensive slow one.
2. **Save gold for the Egg Festival (Spring 13).** ![Strawberry](/images/items/strawberry.png) Strawberries are the best Spring crop.
3. **Build a Silo before your first Coop** so you always have hay.
4. **Sprinklers pay for themselves** in saved time — rush ![Quality Sprinkler](/images/items/quality-sprinkler.png) Quality Sprinklers.
5. **Giant crops** need a 3x3 block of the same crop — Cauliflower, Melon, or Pumpkin.

## Fishing

6. **Buy the Training Rod (25g)** if the minigame frustrates you — bigger bar, easier fish.
7. **Tap, don't hold** the button to keep the green bar steady.
8. **Fish in the rain** — some valuable fish only appear then.
9. **Keep fish for the Community Center** before you sell them all.

## Mining

10. **Use the elevator every 5 floors** — don't try to clear the whole mine in one day.
11. **Bring food** to heal and restore energy while mining.
12. **Mummies need bombs** to stay down (in the Skull Cavern).
13. **Smelt ![Quartz](/images/items/quartz.png) Quartz into Refined Quartz** for Quality Sprinklers and other recipes.

## Money

14. **Artisan goods multiply value.** Turn fruit into wine and vegetables into pickles.
15. **Pigs dig up Truffles** — the most profitable animal product by far.
16. **Repair the bus** to reach the Desert, where Starfruit and the Skull Cavern live.
17. **The Traveling Cart** (Friday/Sunday) sells rare items you'd otherwise wait for.

## Friendship

18. **Never miss a birthday** — birthday gifts are worth 8x friendship.
19. **Carry gems** — they're loved gifts for most villagers.
20. **Talk to villagers daily** to stop friendship decay.

## Quality of life

21. **Check the TV daily** — Fortune Teller (luck), Weather, and recipes.
22. **Upgrade tools in Winter or on rainy days** so you're never stuck without them.
23. **Use the Spa** (opens Summer 3) to fully restore energy for free.
24. **Put chests near your fields** to dump harvests without walking back to the house.
25. **Don't go to bed too late** — sleeping after midnight means less energy the next morning.

## The bottom line

These tips share a theme: **plan ahead, reinvest, and remove friction from your daily routine.** Master a few of them and Stardew Valley gets dramatically easier — and more fun.
