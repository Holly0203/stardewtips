Golden Tags are not guaranteed with every Rainbow Trout. Plan the Trout Derby around repeated catches, then claim what you earn; do not arrive expecting a fixed number of fish to buy a particular reward.

## Where and when to fish

The Derby runs on Summer 20 and 21, from 6:10am until 2am, at the Cindersap Forest river near Marnie's Ranch. Rainbow Trout can be caught there throughout the event regardless of the normal time and weather restrictions. The [Trout Derby reference](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Trout_Derby) explains tag chances and the reward sequence.

Time continues during the event, and animals still need food. Finish essential farm care first. A watered, fed farm makes it easier to stay for another fishing session without wondering what you forgot.

Pack bait before the event rather than relying on a shop stop. Leave several inventory slots for fish, tags, and prizes. If your bag is already full, a good catch can create a sorting interruption at exactly the wrong moment.

## Tags and rewards are different from a shop

A Golden Tag exchanges for the next reward in a sequence; you do not browse a menu and choose any prize. The first reward is a Tent Kit. Tags carry between Derby days and can be kept for a future year, so an unspent tag does not have the same deadline as a Desert Festival egg.

That changes your stopping decision. You can go home when the remaining evening is more valuable elsewhere. You do not need to keep fishing solely to avoid losing a tag overnight.

## Choose equipment that keeps casts moving

If trout are easy to land with your current setup, focus on getting more bites. If you lose fish during the minigame, improving control may help more than a faster bite. See the [bait and tackle guide](/guides/bait-and-tackle-guide/) for those tradeoffs.

A [Bait Maker](/guides/bait-maker-targeted-fishing/) can turn one trout into targeted bait once you have caught it. That uses a saleable fish, so try a small batch rather than sacrificing every catch in advance. Keep track of whether the targeting solves an actual problem in your chosen spot.

Before leaving, visit the booth with free inventory space and collect earned rewards. Save any remaining tags deliberately. For the rest of Summer, compare this short event with your [ordinary fishing goals](/guides/fishing-guide-beginners/) rather than treating the prize ladder as a mandatory two-day assignment.
