Gifts are the fastest friendship lever in Stardew Valley: a loved item is **+80 points** (+640 on a birthday), and hearts unlock recipes, cutscenes, and marriage. You don't need to memorize 30 preference lists, though — half the town loves gems, the other half loves cheap cooking, and one mine run can fund a week of gifting. This is the verified cheat sheet: the easiest loved gift for every villager, with birthdays.

> **At a glance**
>
> - **Two gifts per villager per week**, plus one extra on their birthday — which counts for **8×**.
> - **Gems are the skeleton key**: most of town loves at least one cheap stone from the [mines](/guides/mining-guide-beginners/).
> - Universal loves: **Prismatic Shard, Rabbit's Foot, Golden Pumpkin, Magic Rock Candy, Stardrop Tea, Pearl** — but Haley *hates* Prismatic Shards, so don't hand her your best gem.
> - Universal hates: **all trash** (soggy newspaper, driftwood, Joja Cola) and the **Solar Panel** — worth −40 with everyone.
> - A loved birthday gift (+640) is **2.5 hearts in a single click** — plan your calendar around them.

## The gem strategy

Look at that table again: **nine villagers love a plain gemstone**, and the mines produce hundreds. The efficient loop from our [mining guide](/guides/mining-guide-beginners/):

1. Crack geodes at Clint's (25g each) while mining — amethyst, topaz, aquamarine, jade, and frozen tears pour out.
2. Keep **one stack of cheap gems** in your toolbar instead of selling everything.
3. Hand one out every time you walk past Clint, Emily, Abigail, Sebastian, Maru, or Evelyn.

That's four to six loved gifts a day from an inventory you already have. If you befriend the [Skull Cavern](/guides/skull-cavern-guide/), Prismatic Shards turn the whole town — every villager but Haley — into a +80 gift dispenser.

For artisan gifts, a single [Keg and Preserves Jar setup](/guides/greenhouse-guide/) covers the rest: beer for Shane and Pam, wine for half the valley's birthday list (most villagers like or love wine), cheese for Leah and Robin.

## What never to gift

- **Trash** (driftwood, soggy newspaper, rusty cans, Joja Cola): **−40 with everyone** — and trash from fishing bags is worthless anyway, so it's a free friendship *loss* at no cost saved.
- **Solar Panel**: hated by literally all villagers. A bold museum piece, a terrible gift.
- **Birthday mismatches**: a disliked gift on a birthday is −160. Check the table above before wrapping anything for the Festival crowd.

## The bottom line

Stockpile **cheap gems from the mines**, save one **Rabbit's Foot per birthday**, keep a **Keg running for beer and wine**, and you have a loved gift for every single villager without ever checking a wiki mid-walk — Haley and her Prismatic Shard grudge aside.
