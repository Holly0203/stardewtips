Giving gifts is the fastest way to earn hearts with villagers — and hearts unlock recipes, cutscenes, and even marriage. The trick is knowing what each person *loves*. This guide gives you the easy answers.

## Universal gifts (work on almost everyone)

A few items are loved by nearly every villager:

- ![Prismatic Shard](/images/items/prismatic-shard.png) **Prismatic Shard** — the rarest and most universally loved item.
- ![Rabbit's Foot](/images/items/rabbit-s-foot.png) **Rabbit's Foot** — loved by everyone except Penny.
- **Golden Pumpkin** and **Magic Rock Candy** — rare festival/shop items.

> Rabbit's Foot is the single best "safe" gift in the game. If you're unsure what someone likes, a Rabbit's Foot is almost never wrong.

## What everyone hates

Avoid these — they're universally disliked (or worse):

- **Trash**, **Bug Meat**, **Sap**, and **Joja Cola**.
- Most **artifacts** and **fishing trash**.

Giving a hated gift *lowers* friendship, so when in doubt, give a gem or a Rabbit's Foot instead.

## Tips for gifting efficiently

- **You can give two gifts per week** (resets Sunday). One extra on a villager's birthday.
- **Birthday gifts are worth 8x** friendship — always give a loved gift on birthdays.
- **Quality matters** — higher-quality items give more friendship.
- **Carry a stack of one universal gem** so you always have a gift ready.

## The bottom line

For nearly every villager, the answer is simple: **gems from the mines, Rabbit's Foot, or a universal love**. Learn a handful of favorites, keep gems on hand, and never miss a birthday — and you'll have every villager at max hearts in no time.
